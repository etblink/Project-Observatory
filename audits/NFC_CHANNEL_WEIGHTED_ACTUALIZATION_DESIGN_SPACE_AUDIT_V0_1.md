## Candidate-class adjudication

### W1 structural multiplicity / degeneracy

`RESULT = FAIL_AS_GENERAL_WEIGHT_AUTHORITY`

Multiplicity and representation dimensions are quotient-visible in many contexts, but frozen NFC supplies no theorem that physical actualization probability is proportional to such multiplicity. The trapped-ion adverse targets also show that simple equal-channel counting is false; generic degeneracy counting is not licensed as a repair.

### W2 defect / continuation burden

`RESULT = FAIL__PRIOR_AUTHORITY_AUDIT_CONTROLS`

Defect measures branching burden/order and supports structural bookkeeping. Prior accepted audits established no source-level actualization authority for defect.

### W3 transport / coercive cost

`RESULT = FAIL__CONSTRAINT_NOT_WEIGHT_AUTHORITY`

Transport/coercive quantities constrain lawful persistence and stability but do not acquire probability authority from their role as costs/bounds.

### W4 spectral position / spectral margin

`RESULT = FAIL_AS_GENERAL_CHANNEL_WEIGHT`

SPEC contains spectral data and spectral margins, but spectral location/isolation is not a theorem assigning relative channel probabilities. Two channels at different energies may have arbitrary relative transition strengths consistent with their existence.

### W5 probe-response magnitude / transition-ledger quantity

`RESULT = FAIL_IN_CURRENT_FROZEN_SPEC`

SPEC's transition ledger records certified quotient-visible change and transport/defect bookkeeping. The branch explicitly states that ledger additivity is bookkeeping additivity, not a claim that physical intensities or amplitudes add linearly. No general transition-amplitude or response-strength probability object is presently certified.

### W6 symmetry / representation factor

`RESULT = FAIL_AS_STANDALONE_WEIGHT`

Symmetry can force equalities or exclusions when channels are symmetry-related, but it does not generally determine relative weights among inequivalent lawful channels. A symmetry factor can become part of a future dynamical calculation without itself supplying universal actualization authority.

### W7 branch-local generator or transition-strength object

`RESULT = NOT_CURRENTLY_AVAILABLE_AT_REQUIRED_FORCE`

This is the most promising internal route in form, but the frozen SPEC branch deliberately does not take transition amplitudes as primitives. Its certified generator/response architecture identifies transition-accessible spectral packets and response classes, not a normalized child-specific strength law with empirical probability force.

A future theorem could potentially construct such an object, but that would be new substantive scientific work rather than recovery of an existing weight.

### W8 explicitly new physical weight primitive

`RESULT = LOGICALLY_AVAILABLE__NO_JUSTIFIED_CANDIDATE`

One may postulate a new `W`, but doing so does not count as a successful design-space result absent independent motivation, invariance, falsifiability, and a bridge to the physical target.

### W9 externally derived dynamical quantity with bridge

`RESULT = SURVIVES_AS_ONLY_CURRENTLY CONCRETE ROUTE`

Ordinary spectroscopy already uses child-specific transition rates/strengths to predict nonuniform branching fractions. Such quantities can be fixed independently of the observed outcome frequencies when derived from a physical dynamical model or separate calibration.

However, importing a standard quantum transition-rate rule into NFC would be an external bridge, not a source derivation. This audit does not authorize any particular external rule.

## Why the adverse spectroscopy result matters

The Ca+ and Ba+ systems establish that channel count alone is insufficient. Their sibling channels are independently defined and their frequencies are highly nonuniform. Therefore any stochastic completion intended to cover ordinary spectroscopy must distinguish channels by more than sibling cardinality.

This does not prove that quantum transition amplitudes are the missing NFC actualization law. It proves only that a viable law must be compatible with channel-sensitive dynamics.

## Critical distinction: dynamics versus actualization

There are now two logically separable possibilities:

1. **Dynamics already fixes physical outcome weights.** Actualization merely samples/realizes according to those weights.
2. **Actualization contributes additional weighting beyond dynamics.** Then a separately identifiable deviation from dynamical branching predictions is required.

If case (1) holds, the actualization problem reduces substantially: the missing object is no longer a free measure over lawful children, but a rule explaining why empirically validated dynamical propensities have probability force.

If case (2) holds, the new actualization law must make a distinct empirical prediction beyond ordinary transition dynamics or it is not separately identifiable.

Frozen NFC does not currently decide between these possibilities.

## Empirical identifiability consequence

A future actualization theory that simply sets

`P_i = normalized externally supplied transition rate_i`

may reproduce spectroscopy but risks being only a relabeling of established dynamics. To count as a distinct physical completion, it must either:

- derive those weights from NFC-native structure where external theory previously supplied them; or
- predict a controlled deviation / new regime not already encoded by the imported dynamical law; or
- prove a nontrivial authority theorem explaining why the dynamical weight is the physical actualization measure.

Without one of these, the 'actualization law' adds no independently testable content.
