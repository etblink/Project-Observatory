## Required distinctions

The audit must not conflate:

1. **Outcome architecture** — a finite quotient-visible set of alternatives.
2. **Mathematical normalization** — a rule summing to one.
3. **Dynamical update** — a rule evolving a declared state or population.
4. **Physical transition kernel** — a rule assigning quantitative probabilities to alternative physical outcomes under a fixed preparation/intervention.
5. **Calibration** — determining parameters or channel weights from external data.
6. **Post-hoc fit** — using the very outcome frequencies to be predicted to construct the kernel.

A qualifying derived physical kernel must be prospectively computable from frozen NFC content plus explicitly declared pre-outcome inputs, normalized, representation-invariant at the declared quotient scope, and capable of making quantitative probability predictions before observing the target frequencies.

## Mandatory adversarial tests

1. **Normalization fallacy:** mathematical normalization is not physical probability authority.
2. **Dynamics/probability fallacy:** deterministic or population dynamics are not automatically single-event transition kernels.
3. **Spectral fallacy:** eigenvalues, gaps, intensities, or response margins do not become channel probabilities without a theorem.
4. **Calibration leakage:** any parameter learned from target frequencies disqualifies prospective derivation.
5. **External-law leakage:** standard-model, quantum, statistical, or phenomenological transition laws count as external unless source-descended by an explicit frozen bridge.
6. **Branch-to-source lift:** branch-local kernels do not become universal source laws automatically.
7. **Representation invariance:** raw labels or hidden representatives cannot determine probabilities.
8. **Outcome-partition dependence:** the partition must be prospectively fixed.
9. **Known-data test:** where a branch appears to yield numerical probabilities, ask whether they were independently predicted or merely encoded from known data.
10. **Conservative countermodel:** attempt to preserve all frozen NFC theorem truths while changing the candidate transition probabilities. If possible, source forcing fails.
