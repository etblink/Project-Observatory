## Executive finding

The user's observation-as-selector intuition identifies a real structural hinge in NFC, but the frozen source does not cross from observational selection of quotient-visible distinctions to physical actualization of one history. Internal tests generate observable content, refine observational equivalence, and stabilize quotient classes. Frozen NFC also contains an explicit anti-inverse-selection result at singular quotient interfaces: where distinct configurations are observationally equivalent, no admissible inverse selector may canonically distinguish them. Recursive stabilization therefore closes the observable partition, not the token/history identity hidden inside a many-to-one class.

The result is stronger than keyword absence. The frozen source positively permits multiplicity that observation cannot canonically invert, and SCC independently permits distinct histories with the same structural carrier class after recursive stabilization.

## Evidence map

### 1. Internal tests genuinely generate observation

Book I states that observable content is generated only by admissible internal tests. A licensed internal test family T_n consists of admissible relational processes, and observational equivalence omega ~_n omega' means every licensed test returns the same currently licensed observable outcome (or both are undefined).

Thus the intuition's first premise is correct at frozen scope:

INTERNAL_TEST -> LICENSED_OBSERVABLE_OUTCOME -> OBSERVATIONAL_EQUIVALENCE/QUOTIENT.

### 2. The quotient is classification/compression, not token actualization

Book I defines Sigma_n = Omega / ~_n and later the stabilized quotient Sigma_infty. Survivor chains are explicitly class-level persistence claims, not token-level identity claims, and at Book-I scope have no dynamical or physical interpretation.

The stabilization theorem forces eventual stability of the observational partition. It does not state that every stabilized class is singleton.

Therefore finite recursive refinement gives:

Omega -> Sigma_0 -> Sigma_1 -> ... -> Sigma_infty,

not a theorem of the form

Omega -> omega_actual.

### 3. Decisive anti-selector theorem: singular interfaces

Book I defines a singular projection interface when distinct omega_1, omega_2 map to the same reduced object and no licensed inverse selection rule distinguishes them. It then proves that if omega_1 != omega_2 but omega_1 ~_n omega_2, the canonical quotient map is singular at their shared class.

The accompanying canonical force is explicit: wherever the observational quotient is many-to-one, canonical inverse selection is impossible without illicit discrimination. An admissible inverse selector would contradict observational equivalence.

This is direct positive evidence against the proposition that observation, merely by being NFC observation, source-forces selection of a unique underlying configuration/history.

### 4. Recursive observation does not force singleton convergence

The preregistered filtration idea can be represented at finite stages by the refinement chain of observational equivalence classes. Frozen NFC proves that the chain stabilizes after finitely many stages. But stabilization means Sigma_n = Sigma_N for n >= N; it does not mean every class in Sigma_N has cardinality one.

Indeed the singular-interface theorem expressly covers non-singleton observational classes. Once such a class survives to stabilization, the no-identity-smuggling rule blocks recovering a canonical representative by an admissible test unless new licensed observable structure genuinely separates it.

Hence the canon-forced terminal object is a stabilized observational equivalence class, not a source-forced singleton physical history.

FILTRATION_SINGLETON_FORCE = NO.
OBSERVATIONAL_STABILIZATION = YES.

### 5. Persistent observation remains quotient-visible

Book III defines persistent observables on stabilized quotient classes and requires lawful transfer to preserve quotient structure. Persistent observables are invariant under replacement by quotient-equivalent representatives. Witness-preserving transport therefore propagates certified observable content; it does not restore presentation-level distinctions removed by the quotient.

This makes recursive/persistent observation stronger as a structural filter while preserving the same anti-smuggling boundary.

### 6. SCC supplies a history-level multiplicity witness

The SCC branch's unconditional trichotomy permits confluent distinctness: X not path-equivalent to Y while X cap-equivalent to Y, explicitly described as distinct histories with the same structural carrier class. SCC's transported invariant can be counterfactually distinguishing at the structural level, but the branch does not supply a law choosing one of those histories as physically actual.

This is a constructive anti-selector witness at the history level: recursive structural stabilization and observation-sensitive invariants can coexist with distinct histories.

### 7. Branch legitimacy does not silently add actualization

Book V allows endpoint force to depend on U, the declared realization functor, and declared branch-specific hypotheses, while forbidding undeclared hidden supplementation. Therefore a future observation-triggered actualization law could in principle be introduced only through declared and licensed additional structure. It cannot be read back into the source merely from the fact that a witness/test is observational.

## Filtration verdict

The source licenses a finite refinement filtration of observational classes and forces eventual stabilization. It does not license the stronger theorem

intersection/refinement limit = one physical-history equivalence class.

At frozen scope the correct endpoint is:

RECURSIVE_OBSERVATION -> STABILIZED_OBSERVATIONAL_CLASS,

not

RECURSIVE_OBSERVATION -> UNIQUE_ACTUAL_HISTORY.

## Important nuance

This audit does not show that observation can never be part of an actualization law. It shows that frozen NFC's current witness/test semantics do not themselves supply that law. A future completion could make observation dynamically efficacious, but it would need an explicit source-descended bridge explaining what physical operation the test performs, how alternatives are selected or weighted, and why the rule does not violate the quotient/no-smuggling discipline.

This also means the intuition is scientifically productive: it identifies a constrained completion design space. Any observation-based actualizer must operate on distinctions that are genuinely licensed, and cannot select between canonically indistinguishable representatives by fiat.
