## 6. Physical-neutrality adjudication

The execution is correct that the interface does not erase the physical distinction between deterministic and genuinely stochastic actualization.

Thus the statement

establishes formal embedding, not physical equivalence.

The physical completion problem remains:

- why singleton support? or
- why nontrivial support and these weights?

Frozen NFC answers neither.
