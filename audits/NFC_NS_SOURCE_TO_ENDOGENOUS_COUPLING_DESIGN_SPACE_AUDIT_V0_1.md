## Candidate adjudication

### K1 forced weak/discrete balance insertion
SELECTED / DOMINANT FIRST TARGET.

The candidate theorem would establish, on a prospectively declared forced-NS scope and after lawful source realization, a forced balance of schematic form

partial_t <phi,u_n>
 = [frozen unforced NS Stage-2a terms]
 + <phi,F>_n,

where <phi,F>_n is the lawful finite/refining representative of the basis-free source pairing and reduces to zero when the exogenous source vanishes.

This candidate is attractive because:
- it uses the same test-function interface already carrying the unforced balance;
- source remains a separate additive channel rather than being retyped as defect;
- zero source recovers the frozen balance exactly;
- basis/dictionary dependence can be removed by the already accepted reconstruction-equivalence machinery;
- work/energy injection can become a downstream test choice or consequence rather than the primitive bridge;
- the OpenAI forced construction can later serve as a direct adversarial target because its force is supplied in the same continuum weak-equation role.

### K2 energy/work injection primitive

A term analogous to <F,u> is physically relevant but state-dependent and scalarized. The frozen Galerkin/energy architecture already obtains energy estimates by suitable test choices. Therefore work injection should be derived from the forced balance when the required test choice is lawful, not introduced as the foundational source bridge.
