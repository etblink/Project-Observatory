## BIO review

PASS WITH IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE.

BIO contains a genuine normalized update:

`p_{n+1}([X]) = p_n([X]) N_1([X]) / sum_[Y] p_n([Y]) N_1([Y])`.

This must not be dismissed as merely formal. It is a real conditional population-composition dynamics derived from differential replication rates within BIO's declared scope.

However, the audited question is whether frozen NFC supplies a prospectively calibrated **single-event physical transition kernel over alternative outcomes**. BIO's equation evolves a population distribution; its `replication kernel` is a certified copy relation/set, not a stochastic event kernel. The execution therefore correctly classifies BIO as K2 rather than K4.

If a future bridge theorem identifies a population update with an individual-event sampling process, that would be new theorem content and should be audited separately.

PASS.

The countermodel construction is sufficient at audited scope: hold fixed the frozen quotient-visible alternatives, admissibility constraints, branch equations, conserved quantities, transfer structure and endpoint claims; vary only the numerical probability weights among alternatives where the frozen theory leaves them unspecified. Therefore the missing probability weights are not forced by the audited frozen theory.

This does not prove that no future theorem from an expanded NFC could derive them. It proves only that the currently frozen theorem corpus does not.
