## Executive finding

Frozen NFC does contain a reusable noncircular governance schema by which a future actualization law could acquire canonical physical authority. The schema is already encoded in Books V and VII: certified source content, explicit realization/descent, branch visibility, a legitimacy witness, no hidden supplementation, and—when force is promoted from source-side structure to physical endpoint interpretation—a separately proved transfer theorem.

However, the frozen source does not currently contain the scientific actualization content that such a transfer theorem would carry. Governance can certify and police an authority bridge; it cannot manufacture the missing physical premise.

## Existing authority-transfer architecture

### Book V: lawful descent and legitimacy

Book V requires a lawful branch to be sourced through the universal source object and declared descent machinery. A legitimacy witness must establish that endpoint content arises by certified descent, is branch-visible, and requires no undeclared supplementary structure beyond the source, the declared realization functor, and openly declared branch-specific hypotheses.

This gives a ready-made anti-smuggling framework for an actualization candidate.

### Book VII: promotion requires transfer

Book VII defines a bridge theorem as a theorem carrying force from one scoped regime to another. It states that a scoped certificate does not automatically extend beyond its regime and that promotion requires both certified source content and a separately proved transfer theorem.

Crucially, Book VII explicitly records the corollary:

`source-side canonicality does not self-promote to physical endpoint identification.`

This is exactly the distinction exposed by the actualization audits.

## Candidate authority-bridge schema

A future NFC actualization-authority proof could lawfully have the following form:

1. **Source certificate S**: prove on a declared source/post-source regime a concrete structural law `Q_NFC(Z,h)` with exact hypotheses and scope.
2. **Actualization target declaration**: define a target object containing lawful post-observation states Z, continuation spaces H(Z), and a typed actualization map `A_Z`.
3. **Bridge theorem T_A**: separately prove that the certified source content S has physical actualization force in the target regime, yielding constraints on or a value for `A_Z`.
4. **Realization/descent witness**: show the target construction is sourced through U and the declared realization machinery.
5. **Quotient-visibility / anti-inversion**: prove the bridge does not select hidden representatives inside observational equivalence classes.
6. **Representation invariance**: prove source-equivalent presentations induce equivalent actualization assignments.
7. **No hidden supplementation**: expose every auxiliary hypothesis; no undeclared random seed, preferred basis, hidden identity, boundary choice, branch choice, or probability interpretation may enter.
8. **Promotion discipline**: keep the result conditional or scoped unless a transfer theorem genuinely carries it farther.
9. **Failure frontier**: state the exact conditions under which authority is lost or the bridge fails.

This is a noncircular proof schema because physical actuality need not appear in the premises of S. The burden is instead placed on T_A: it must prove why source-certified structure has physical actualization force.

## Adversarial tests

### Declared-hypothesis test

A selector can be openly declared as a branch-specific hypothesis and still satisfy honesty/governance requirements. This makes it a lawful conditional model, not a source-forced selector. Therefore declaration alone does not establish authority.

### Realization-functor test

A realization functor can transport or expose declared structure, but hiding a selector inside the functor would merely relocate the assumption. The legitimacy witness/no-hidden-channel rules correctly detect this unless the selector is explicitly declared and separately licensed.

### Branch-legitimacy test

Book V can distinguish lawful transparent import from hidden import, but it cannot by itself distinguish a merely declared selector from a source-derived selector. That distinction depends on the selector's dependency chain and the existence of a transfer theorem.

### Transfer test

PASS for schema feasibility.

Book VII explicitly provides the constitutional pattern: certified source content plus a separately proved transfer theorem can promote force to a new target regime. Therefore NFC governance does not forbid an actualization authority bridge in principle.

### Governance test

Governance is necessary but not sufficient. Book VII states that it regulates theorem force and does not add new source or endpoint content. Therefore governance can specify what a valid actualization proof must look like but cannot supply the missing physical law.

### Conservative-extension test

PASS for the conclusion that the scientific premise is missing.

Two openly declared incompatible actualization laws could each be housed in transparent branch-specific conditional packages and remain governable under Book V, provided each exposes its assumptions and passes its own legitimacy tests. Governance therefore does not choose between them. A source-side scientific certificate plus actualization transfer theorem is still required to elevate one from honest model choice to source-supported physical authority.

## What is missing

The missing object is more precise than before:

`SOURCE-SIDE ACTUALIZATION-RELEVANT CERTIFICATE S`

plus

`ACTUALIZATION TRANSFER THEOREM T_A : S -> physical force on A_Z`.

Current NFC quantities can serve as candidate inputs to S, but the prior authority audit established that none presently carries source-level actualization semantics.

## Why Outcome D is not justified

The audit does not establish that an irreducible new physical postulate is logically necessary. A future theorem might derive an actualization-relevant certificate from existing source structure that has not yet been recognized or proved. Therefore the stronger conclusion `AUTHORITY_BRIDGE_REQUIRES_IRREDUCIBLE_NEW_PHYSICAL_POSTULATE` would exceed the current evidence.

What is established is that the frozen canon has the governance schema but lacks the scientific premise/transfer proof.
