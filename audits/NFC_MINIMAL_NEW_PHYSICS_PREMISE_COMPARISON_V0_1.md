## Criterion-by-criterion comparison

### 1. Added physical structure

**S advantage.**

S adds one explicit physical claim: lawful siblings have equal actualization weight.

D requires, at minimum, both a ranking structure and authority for that ranking to actualize. Writing these as one sentence — e.g. `the actual child is the unique minimizer of Q` — does not remove either commitment; it only packages them together. The choice of `Q` remains physical structure.

### 2. NFC representation discipline

**Tie.**

Both can be made quotient-visible and representation-invariant.

S operates on child classes themselves rather than hidden representatives.

D can also satisfy no-smuggling if its ranking functional is defined only on quotient-visible structure. Any raw-label lexicography is disallowed.

### 3. Mathematical closure

**S advantage at current maturity.**

On every finite lawful child set, S defines a normalized next-step kernel.

D is total only if its ranking has a unique winner at every branching node or if ties have an explicit lawful resolution. Frozen NFC does not guarantee this.

### 4. Projective/sequential consistency

**S advantage.**

The local rule `1/k(C)` composes into normalized cylinder measures by multiplication and is automatically projectively consistent under finite marginalization.

D is sequentially coherent only after a total deterministic choice function is supplied. Such a function is not presently source-given.

### 5. Tie/degeneracy burden

**S strong advantage.**

S has no tie problem: all lawful children receive defined weight.

D faces genuine degeneracy whenever multiple children share the proposed extremal value or ranking data. A hidden tie-breaker would violate the source discipline.

### 6. Parameter burden

**S advantage for the minimal candidate.**

S1 introduces no tunable numerical parameter.

A deterministic rule may also be parameter-free in principle, but no currently available total quotient-visible selector has that status. Candidate functionals can introduce thresholds, horizons, weights, or ordering choices.

### 7. Failure exposure

**Both pass, with different failure modes.**

S fails if empirical frequencies or intervention statistics systematically violate equal sibling weighting in a domain where the sibling classes and auxiliary policy are independently fixed.

D fails if the ranking is non-total, produces ties not resolved by theorem, or generates observably wrong selected outcomes.

S is easier to falsify cleanly because its first candidate is more rigid.

### 8. Empirical discriminator potential

**No current winner.**

Neither package is yet linked to a concrete empirical regime with calibrated frequencies/interventions. Both can become empirically distinct once a target system and observation map are specified.

S may expose frequency predictions more directly; D may expose event-selection predictions more directly. This does not presently rank truth.

### 9. Branch compatibility

**Tie at present design scope.**

Prior ten-branch stress testing found no frozen branch theorem whose truth requires the absence of either completion class, provided scope and provenance are respected.

### 10. Auxiliary sensitivity

**S advantage.**

The local equal-child rule is horizon-free and uses no initial ranking horizon or hidden ordering.

The cleanest current D candidates depend on choosing a ranking functional, and cumulative-defect variants may additionally depend on a future window unless a horizon-free local functional is found.

This critique **succeeds as an authority objection** but does not defeat S as a candidate. S1 is explicitly declared as the new physical premise rather than disguised as a theorem.

## D-reversal

Could D1 and D2 collapse into one premise?

A statement such as

`Actual(C) = unique argmin Q(C_i)`

is syntactically one postulate but semantically contains two independent commitments:

1. `Q` is the physically relevant ranking among lawful children;
2. its unique extremum has actualization authority.

Changing `Q` changes the physical theory while retaining the same extremization syntax. Therefore ranking content cannot be eliminated merely by combining wording.

In addition, the postulate is partial wherever the minimum is non-unique unless a tie theorem or tie rule is added.

`D_REVERSAL = FAIL_TO_MATCH_S_MINIMALITY_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE`
