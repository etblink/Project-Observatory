## 3. Frozen primary question

> What is the minimum source-bound mathematical object needed to cross the admissibility/generation → physical selection boundary in NFC and in PGH, which requirements are genuinely shared, and which are project-specific?

The operation must not collapse:

- **NFC actualization:** selecting or weighting actual history/histories among admissible histories;
- **PGH law exhaustion:** making substantive physical possibility arise from grammar rather than from an independent selector law `L`.

## 4. Required distinctions

The execution must keep separate:

1. `ADMISSIBILITY` — membership in a lawful/well-formed class;
2. `EXCLUSION` — ruling out some candidates;
3. `CANONICALIZATION` — selecting a representation/equivalence class or normal form;
4. `DYNAMICS` — a transition/evolution rule on states or histories;
5. `POSSIBILITY_SELECTION` — determining which well-formed structures count as physically possible;
6. `HISTORY_ACTUALIZATION` — determining which physically possible/admissible history is actual, or assigning an endogenous probability/weighting law over histories;
7. `EMPIRICAL_INSTANTIATION` — assigning formal roles to real records/systems.

No step may be inferred from another without explicit source force.

## 5. NFC-side qualifying object

For NFC, a qualifying actualization mechanism must do more than constrain admissibility, define dynamics, distinguish histories, canonicalize carriers, or classify equivalent histories.

It must provide at least one source-bound mechanism such as:

- deterministic choice of one actual history/class from multiple admissible alternatives;
- endogenous normalized probability/measure/kernel over admissible histories with physical actualization force;
- source-forced variational/extremal principle that selects realized history/class;
- unique-history theorem whose selector hypotheses are themselves source-forced;
- equivalent noncircular actualization law.

## 6. PGH-side qualifying object

For Strong PGH, a qualifying law-exhaustion mechanism need not choose one unique actual history.

It must instead establish that substantive physical possibility is determined by grammar-generated structure/equivalence without an independent substantive selector `L` doing the physical-selection work.

A qualifying mechanism must therefore provide:

- a grammar-generated nontrivial physical possibility restriction;
- representation-resistant physical force rather than formal exclusion alone;
- a non-smuggled semantic/physical bridge;
- no independent substantive law relocated into primitives, topology, scope, bridge, interpretation, initial structure, or target-specific rules;
- enough physical significance to replace the relevant role of `Select_L` at audited scope.

## 7. Candidate shared core

The execution must test, not assume, whether both projects require the following common selector-core properties:

`C1 DOMAIN_INDEPENDENCE` — candidate alternatives are defined before selection.

`C2 NONTRIVIAL_SELECTIVITY` — the mechanism distinguishes/rules among alternatives rather than merely renaming them.

`C3 ENDOGENEITY` — selection force arises from the audited source/grammar rather than an auxiliary substantive selector.

`C4 NONCIRCULARITY` — the desired physical result is not inserted into the selector assumptions.

`C5 REPRESENTATION_INVARIANCE` — selection force survives faithful presentation changes at the claimed scope.

`C6 SEMANTIC_PHYSICAL_FORCE` — the selector has physical, not merely formal, consequence.

`C7 PROVENANCE_VISIBILITY` — every load-bearing selective input is explicitly charged.

`C8 MULTIPLICITY_RESOLUTION` — the mechanism resolves the particular multiplicity relevant to the project rather than merely classifying it.

The audit must determine whether all eight are genuinely shared and whether either project requires additional conditions.
