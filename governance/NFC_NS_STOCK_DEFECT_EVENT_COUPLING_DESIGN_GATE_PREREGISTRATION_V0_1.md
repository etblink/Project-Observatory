## Question
What is the weakest noncircular event/provenance architecture capable of relating the memory-bearing interface stock S_k to the current visible/successor-defect state J_k (and Z_k=mathfrakD_k conditionally), strongly enough to expose the signed obstruction drift

Delta O_k = Delta S_k - Delta J_k

without assuming the desired obstruction contraction?

## Frozen/accepted inputs
- Canonical weighted boundary split atoms for stepwise boundary generation.
- Typed witness lineages and quotient-image status for active interface stock.
- Exact active witness-mass evolution with quotient loss, migration, merge/dedup loss, and effective new generation.
- Canonical Book-III <-> SCT mass-preserving crosswalk for current internal/boundary defect channels.
- Direct obstruction recurrence is nonforced because persistent stock can coexist with falling current defect.

## Candidate architectures
C1 SAME-EVENT IDENTITY: identify current defect atoms with active-stock witnesses. Control candidate; reject if internal defects or temporal typing prevent identity.

C2 BOUNDARY-ONLY LINEAGE COUPLING: map boundary defect atoms into active-stock lineages while leaving internal defect separate.

C3 FINITE SIGNED EVENT-FLOW LEDGER: retain distinct event classes and assign their exact signed contribution to Delta S and Delta J, including retained stock, stock resolution/loss, migration, merge, effective generation, current defect loss/renewal, and adaptive-window discrepancy. This is bookkeeping only unless an independent sign/bias theorem is available.

C4 AGGREGATE EXPOSURE INEQUALITY: directly posit a bound on (Delta S-Delta J). Reject as circular if it merely restates the desired O recurrence without source-derived event content.

C5 UNIFORM STOCK TURNOVER: every active interface witness resolves within a fixed number/rate of steps. Treat as new dynamics unless derived.

C6 AGE-STRUCTURED STOCK: enrich active witnesses by age and derive turnover from finite-state lineage semantics if possible.

C7 STOCHASTIC HAZARD/TURNOVER: reject unless probability structure is independently licensed.

C8 NO NONCIRCULAR COUPLING ARCHITECTURE.

## Mandatory tests
1. Internal defect has no interface-stock identity by type.
2. Boundary generation may duplicate existing active stock and must be deduplicated.
3. Stock loss by migration is not necessarily physical defect destruction.
4. Quotient-image loss and merge are genuine active-stock mass loss but require crosswalk before identifying them with H1/H2 loss channels.
5. Falling J contributes positively to Delta O unless S falls at least comparably; sign must be explicit.
6. New stock generation contributes positively to Delta O; current defect generation contributes negatively through -Delta J and may not be silently canceled.
7. Window discrepancy remains separately signed.
8. No probability/hazard semantics without a licensed probability bridge.
9. No static S~J proportionality.
10. No assumption of O contraction inside the coupling premise.
