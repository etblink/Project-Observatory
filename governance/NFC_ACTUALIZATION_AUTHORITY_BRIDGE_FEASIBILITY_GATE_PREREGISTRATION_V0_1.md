## Question

## Scope

This gate does not choose an actualization law. It asks whether NFC contains enough governance structure to state what a legitimate authority bridge would have to prove.

## Required bridge conditions

A viable authority bridge must, at minimum:

1. be source-descended or otherwise explicitly licensed under NFC governance;
2. act only on quotient-visible / canonically meaningful structure;
3. avoid inverse selection inside an observational equivalence class;
4. expose all additional hypotheses and auxiliary structure;
5. connect a lawful post-observation state Z and its continuation set H(Z) to an actualization map A_Z;
6. explain why A_Z has physical rather than merely mathematical or representational force;
7. remain noncircular: physical actuality may not be assumed in the premises used to prove physical actuality;
8. be invariant under source-equivalent presentations;
9. declare scope, failure conditions, and transfer requirements;
10. satisfy Book-V/Book-VII no-hidden-upgrade and promotion discipline.

- `DECLARED_HYPOTHESIS_TEST`: merely declaring A_Z as a branch hypothesis is not enough to prove source-derived authority.
- `REALIZATION_FUNCTOR_TEST`: a realization functor may carry an actualization law only if the law's authority is itself licensed, not smuggled into F.
