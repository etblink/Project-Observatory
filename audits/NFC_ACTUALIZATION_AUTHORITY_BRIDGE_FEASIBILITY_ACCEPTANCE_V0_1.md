## Independent review

The execution correctly distinguishes three different things that must not be conflated:

1. **governance legitimacy** — whether a selector is openly declared, source-descended where claimed, branch-visible, and free of hidden supplementation;
2. **transfer legitimacy** — whether certified content can lawfully be promoted from one scoped regime to another through a separately proved transfer theorem;
3. **scientific actualization authority** — why a particular source-side structure should have physical force over the actualization map `A_Z`.

Books V and VII supply the first two layers. They do not presently supply the third.

## Book-V check

PASS.

Book V explicitly permits declared branch-specific hypotheses inside a legitimacy witness so long as the endpoint depends only on the source, the declared realization functor, and openly declared hypotheses. Therefore a selector can be housed honestly as a conditional model without becoming source-forced merely by being declared.

Book V's no-hidden-channel rule is exactly the right anti-smuggling test for future actualization work, but it does not manufacture the selector's physical authority.

## Book-VII check

PASS.

Book VII explicitly states that a scoped certificate carries no automatic force beyond its regime and that promotion requires a separately proved transfer theorem. It also explicitly records that source-side canonicality does not self-promote to physical endpoint identification.

This establishes a real constitutional route for a future actualization bridge while simultaneously blocking illicit self-promotion.

## Noncircularity check

PASS.

The accepted schema does not assume actual physical history in the source certificate. Instead it requires:

`source certificate S + actualization transfer theorem T_A -> physical force on A_Z`.

The theorem `T_A` bears the burden of explaining why source-certified structure has actualization force. If `T_A` simply assumes the desired selector or probability law, the proof is circular and fails the schema.

## Conservative-extension check

PASS.

Different openly declared actualization laws can in principle be governed as separate conditional branch/model structures while preserving the same frozen source truths. Therefore governance alone cannot select among them.

This supports the conclusion that the missing object is scientific content, not governance machinery.

## Why Outcome D remains unproved

The frozen corpus does not currently establish that a wholly new irreducible physical postulate is necessary. It remains logically possible that a not-yet-proved theorem from existing source primitives could supply an actualization-relevant certificate.

Therefore the stronger outcome `AUTHORITY_BRIDGE_REQUIRES_IRREDUCIBLE_NEW_PHYSICAL_POSTULATE` is not yet licensed.

## Accepted frontier

The actualization problem is now decomposed into two missing scientific objects:

1. `S_A` — an actualization-relevant source certificate that is stronger than ordinary admissibility/order/stability but does not already assume the final selector;
2. `T_A` — a transfer theorem carrying `S_A` into physical authority over `A_Z`.

The governance stack that would certify such a construction already exists.
