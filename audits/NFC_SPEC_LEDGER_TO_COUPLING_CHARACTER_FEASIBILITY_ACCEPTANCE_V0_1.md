The execution correctly treats Book IV's transport-invariant normalization as genuine canonical source structure. The coefficients `(1,1,beta)` and `beta=log_2(3/2)` are not arbitrary conventions at their declared transport-ledger scope.

## YM heat-semigroup review

PASS.

The YM use of `exp(-beta Delta_A)` is a real branch-specific precedent showing that canonical source normalization can enter a downstream positive operator. But its theorem-bearing role is tied to the YM covariant Laplacian, Perron-Frobenius/vacuum uniqueness, spectral gap, and clustering.

There is no frozen bridge identifying a generic SPEC probe ledger with `Delta_A`, or a generic SPEC probe with the heat-semigroup generator. Therefore no SPEC coupling law follows.

## Character-family review

PASS WITH DOMAIN QUALIFICATION.

The audit's exponential family argument is illustrative, not a claim that every SPEC ledger globally supplies one scalar coordinate. Where an additive scalar ledger component or lawful scalar projection exists, composition admits a family of multiplicative characters. Where the ledger is genuinely multi-component or only partially ordered, even more representation freedom remains.

Thus the negative conclusion does not depend on one globally preferred scalarization. It is enough that composition/additivity by themselves do not select a unique physical scalar representation.

## Beta-selection review

PASS.

Fixing `beta` cannot solve the whole problem because at least two independent burdens remain:

1. which episode/ledger quantity is to be scalarized; and
2. why the selected scalar transform has coupling/amplitude authority.

Hence the canonical defect-ledger unit removes, at most, a normalization freedom inside its declared source role. It does not create the missing physical interaction semantics.
