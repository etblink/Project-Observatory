## Question

Does the frozen NFC/SPEC process architecture admit a source-licensed representation functor from an appropriate category of admissible SPEC probe processes into an operator arena, preserving the already accepted relational/ledger action, without importing physical coupling coefficients, probabilities, amplitudes, or hidden state labels?

Candidate form:

`F_rep : Proc_SPEC -> OpArena_SPEC`

with `F_rep(P)=V_P` and typed composition preserved wherever source composition is defined.

## Required distinction

This gate tests **operator representability**, not physical interaction authority. A positive result does not imply that `V_P` is a physical perturbation, does not determine transition strengths, and does not license a Born rule, Hamiltonian coupling, or probability kernel.

## Candidate representation levels

The audit must distinguish at least:

- R0: Boolean/relational representation already accepted.
- R1: free algebraic representation on quotient-visible classes or episode paths.
- R2: ledger-preserving linear representation after a declared scalar/ring choice.
- R3: representation into the existing SPEC operator packet/algebra.
- R4: representation into a YM/SM physical operator arena.

A theorem at R1 or R2 may be mathematically canonical only relative to an explicitly chosen target category; such conditional canonicity must not be mislabeled as source selection of that category.

## Mandatory tests

1. **Typed-composition test** — determine whether source processes form an ordinary category, partial category, multicategory, or other typed action structure adequate for functorial representation.
2. **Quotient-well-definedness test** — observationally equivalent presentations must not acquire distinct operators solely from hidden labels.
3. **Episode multiplicity test** — distinct lawful episodes with the same quotient-visible endpoints must remain distinct unless source theorems license identification.
4. **Identity/unit test** — do not assume the formal identity extension is a licensed observational probe merely because it can serve as a composition unit.
5. **Faithfulness test** — identify whether a representation can preserve the accepted support/ledger semantics without false distinctions or false identifications.
6. **Target-choice test** — separate existence of a representation into some algebra from source selection of a unique operator arena.
7. **Scalar-choice test** — no hidden choice of R, C, positivity, phase, norm, or inner product may be treated as source-derived unless independently licensed.
8. **Generator-compatibility test** — SPEC's statement that probes exercise modes of `L_SPEC` must not be upgraded to `P -> V_P` unless a theorem actually supplies that assignment.
9. **Representation-layer test** — Book IV's initially empty representation layer must be respected; population requires a lawful bridge.
10. **Conservative-nonuniqueness test** — construct inequivalent representations preserving the same accepted relational/ledger semantics where possible.
11. **Restricted-subclass test** — search for a nontrivial source-selected subclass of probes for which operator representation is stronger than for the generic class.
12. **Physical-authority firewall** — even a unique algebraic representation does not become a physical interaction law without a separate physical bridge.

## Strongest positive target

Construct the strongest representation theorem available from frozen structure. Preference is given to a representation preserving:

- typed composition;
- quotient visibility;
- episode identity/provenance;
- support/reachability;
- additive ledger composition;
- source-equivalent presentation invariance.

If only a free/universal representation exists after choosing a target algebraic category, state its universal property and exactly which choices remain external.

## Strongest negative target

Construct a nonforcing argument showing two inequivalent operator/algebraic representations of the same source-forced probe/ledger action, or show that no fixed-endomorphism target is even type-correct for the generic process class without additional completion data.
