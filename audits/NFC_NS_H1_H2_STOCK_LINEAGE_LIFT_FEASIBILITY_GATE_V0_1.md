## Finding
Frozen H1/H2 mechanisms can be recognized on active interface-stock lineages when their stated predicates are met, but frozen NFC does not force a positive fraction of the full active-stock mass to satisfy those predicates on every relevant step/window.

### H1
H1 concerns non-invariant boundary/interface mismatch under quotient-visible transfer. Its quantitative c1 argument erases mismatch content that fails to remain quotient-visible and assigns it to the null sector.

Active interface-stock witnesses are, by construction, certified quotient-visible distinctions currently contributing to interface burden. This does not imply that every such witness is non-invariant under the next lawful transfer. A witness may remain quotient-visible and boundary-active under identity/boundary-preserving transfer. When an active witness does become non-invariant and loses quotient-image mass, that event is canonically recognizable as active-stock quotient-image loss L^q. But no source theorem proves a uniform positive active-stock fraction in that class.

Therefore L1 FULL H1 LIFT fails, and no bound L^q >= c_1 M is derived.

### H2
H2 concerns finite-collar mixing of incompatible boundary-mismatch patterns. If two active stock lineages are collar-incompatible and collide onto one target distinction, the event is canonically a merge/dedup stock loss C^merge. However a current active-stock population may be concentrated in one compatible/stable collar type or otherwise contain no source-forced positive fraction of incompatible pairs.

Thus H2 gives an eventwise mechanism lift but not C^merge >= c_2 M or any universal positive stock-mixing rate.

### Identity-transfer control
The existing exact stock recurrence admits identity/boundary-preserving transfer with no generation and no certified stock loss. H1 does not rule this out for transfer-invariant quotient-visible active content, and H2 does not rule it out absent incompatible-pair mixing. Therefore the control remains admissible at the present source scope.

## Candidate adjudication
L1 FULL H1 LIFT — REJECTED.
L2 PARTIAL H1 LIFT — EVENT-DEFINED SUBSET YES, but no positive source-forced fraction.
L3 FULL H2 LIFT — REJECTED.
L4 CONDITIONAL H2 LIFT — ACCEPTED eventwise for incompatible active lineages, with no population fraction.
L5 EVENTWISE MECHANISM LIFT ONLY — ACCEPTED / MAXIMAL SOURCE-SUPPORTED RESULT.
L6 NO LIFT — TOO PESSIMISTIC.

## Consequence
The missing ingredient has sharpened again. One does not need a new definition of quotient loss or mixing. What is missing is a theorem forcing enough of the active interface-stock population into non-invariant/incompatible classes to produce a uniform stock-resolution rate.

Provisionally:

T_NS-ACTIVE-STOCK-NONINVARIANCE/TURNOVER.

Such a theorem must be deterministic/quotient-visible unless a separate probability bridge is supplied. It must not infer turnover merely from finiteness of the collar alphabet, and it must permit invariant protected content where the source allows it.
