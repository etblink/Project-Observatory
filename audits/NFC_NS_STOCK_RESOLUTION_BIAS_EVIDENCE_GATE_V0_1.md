## Evidence result
Frozen NFC supplies real mechanism-level overlap between the current visible-ledger loss theorems and the post-freeze active-stock witness construction, but it does not supply the quantitative population crosswalk needed for T_NS-STOCK-RESOLUTION-BIAS.

### 1. Quotient loss
Frozen NS H1 states that boundary/interface mismatch on the safe tail is non-persistent under quotient-visible transfer and certifies a quotient-loss contribution Phi^quot with a positive lower rate relative to the branch defect state.

The active-stock construction independently defines L^q as the loss of weighted quotient-image cardinality along transported interface-witness lineages.

These are the same kind of quotient-visible erasure mechanism. When an H1-erased mismatch is also an active interface-stock lineage, the event contributes to L^q. However frozen H1 does not identify its entire loss population with the active interface-stock population, nor does it lower-bound L^q by c_1 times active stock. Therefore

H1 mechanism -> possible L^q event attribution,

but not

L^q >= c_1 M

or any equivalent stock-resolution rate.

### 2. Finite-collar mixing
Frozen H2 states that incompatible boundary-mismatch patterns undergo finite-collar mixing/collision, with a positive per-window rate c_2 against the branch defect state.

The active-stock construction defines C^merge as excess active mass removed when distinct transported lineages deduplicate onto an identical target distinction.

A collision of two active stock lineages that lands on one quotient-visible target distinction is exactly a C^merge event. Thus there is a genuine event-level overlap. But H2 does not prove that the mismatch patterns whose collisions realize the c_2 lower bound exhaust, or even uniformly sample, the active interface-stock lineage population. Hence no bound

C^merge >= c_2 M

is source-derived.

### 3. Migration
X^mig is certified mass leaving the target interface while remaining lawfully transported elsewhere. No frozen H1/H2 theorem identifies this with defect destruction or current-ledger loss. It is a stock-exit channel and may help reduce S, but it is not part of the present H1/H2 loss crosswalk.

### 4. Generation
The exact witness-mass result already proves

0 <= G^eff <= B_n,

because newly generated boundary atoms are deduplicated against transported active target distinctions before increasing stock. The targeted Book-III/SCT crosswalk identifies B_n with the corresponding boundary visible-ledger generation channel at the finite-balance level. This is a real one-sided quantitative crosswalk on the generation side.

### 5. Net bias
The direct obstruction route requires control of the signed balance between stock resolution/exit and mechanisms that reduce the current ledger. Existing evidence does not prove that active-stock quotient/merge loss keeps pace with Phi^quot+Phi^mix or with additional visible-ledger decrease slack sigma. The certified H1/H2 constants are rates against the current branch defect state, not the active witness stock.

Therefore the net inequality needed for

-R^S + Phi + sigma

to be dissipative is not source-forced.
