## Adversarial tests

### Normalization fallacy

PASS. Book-I child counts, entropy, defect, and branch quantities admit many mathematical normalizations. Prior ELCAK provided an explicit example. Mathematical normalization alone did not survive empirical spectroscopy and therefore cannot count as physical kernel authority.

### Dynamics/probability fallacy

PASS. BIO's normalized population update is the strongest counterexample: it is a real dynamical law at its scope but is not a probability law for which individual physical history actualizes. GR/NS/YM likewise constrain/evolve branch states without producing stochastic single-event kernels.

### Spectral fallacy

PASS. SPEC/YM spectral objects provide modes, gaps, margins and response classes. SPEC explicitly refuses to treat transition amplitudes as primitive and explicitly limits ledger additivity to bookkeeping. No probability theorem follows from spectral structure alone.

### Calibration leakage

PASS. No candidate was upgraded to K4 by inserting empirically measured branching fractions, fitted amplitudes, observed rates, or outcome frequencies.

### External-law leakage

PASS. Standard quantum transition-rate laws, Born-rule weights, Fermi-golden-rule rates, oscillator strengths, and analogous external physical laws are not counted as frozen NFC derivations unless a frozen bridge derives them. No such frozen bridge was identified.

### Branch-to-source lift

PASS. Even BIO's conditional selection dynamics remains branch-local and cannot become a universal source transition law.

### Representation invariance

PASS. No kernel relying on hidden token labels or inverse selection inside observational equivalence classes was admitted.

### Outcome-partition dependence

PASS. Prior ELCAK work established that probabilities depend on the prospectively fixed quotient-visible partition. This audit does not infer physical weights from a post-hoc partition.

### Known-data test

PASS. No observed branching fractions or other frequencies were treated as derived predictions merely because a branch can encode them as response/intensity data.

## Constructive nonforcing argument

The result is stronger than failure to locate a formula.

Fix any frozen NFC regime/branch in which multiple quotient-visible physical alternatives are lawful and in which the frozen theorem statements are satisfied. Suppose no frozen theorem assigns numerical probabilities to those alternatives.

Construct two extensions:

- `M_1`: attach normalized kernel `K_1` over the same lawful alternatives;
- `M_2`: attach a different normalized kernel `K_2 != K_1` over the same alternatives.

Require both kernels to preserve the frozen admissibility set, quotient partition, branch-visible observables, deterministic branch constraints, conservation laws, transfer structure, and declared endpoint equations.

Because the audited frozen theorems constrain the alternative space and its lawful dynamics/structure but do not reference the missing probability weights, both extensions preserve their theorem truth while predicting different frequencies.

Therefore the frozen theorem corpus does not force the quantitative transition probabilities.

Where a branch already contains a deterministic/population update, the same argument applies to any additional single-event stochastic kernel unless the branch theorem explicitly identifies that kernel with the update. No such universal identification is frozen.
