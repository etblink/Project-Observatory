## Mandatory adversarial tests

- **Adjacency fallacy:** a 0/1 real matrix is not automatically a physical operator.
- **Inner-product fallacy:** existence of an ambient bilinear/Hilbert structure does not by itself determine a probe interaction operator.
- **Spectral-label fallacy:** identifying accessible eigenmodes does not determine coefficients coupling the parent/probe to those modes.
- **Path-counting fallacy:** ordinary linear matrix multiplication counts multiplicities that Boolean relational composition suppresses; multiplicity must be physically licensed.
- **RH transfer fallacy:** RH's Mellin/scaling weights and half-density normalization are branch-specific unless a SPEC bridge is separately proved.
- **LING transfer fallacy:** grammar/composition can establish typed action without physical amplitude authority.
- **Matter-extension fallacy:** enlarging the Hilbert/operator packet does not by itself define a probe-dependent interaction term.
- **Gauge-normalization fallacy:** intrinsic normalization of YM root/operator structure does not automatically normalize transition amplitudes.
- **Retrofitting fallacy:** no use of known branching fractions to infer the missing bridge.
