## Candidate-class review

PASS.

No frozen NFC-native candidate simultaneously supplies child-specific prospective values, normalization, noncircularity, and physical probability authority.

- multiplicity/degeneracy: structurally meaningful but no general probability authority;
- defect: bookkeeping/ordering, previously rejected as actualization authority;
- transport/coercive cost: constraint rather than probability law;
- spectral position/margin: does not determine relative transition likelihood;
- SPEC transition ledger: certified change bookkeeping, explicitly not a physical-amplitude additivity theorem;
- symmetry: may force equalities among symmetry-related channels but does not solve inequivalent-channel weights;
- branch-local generator strength: not presently certified as a normalized child-specific probability object.

## External-dynamics route review

PASS WITH FIREWALL.

Ordinary spectroscopy supplies independently specifiable channel-sensitive dynamical quantities capable of predicting nonuniform branching. Such a route can repair the raw equal-child failure in model form, but importing it into NFC requires a separately declared bridge and does not count as source derivation.

No particular external quantum rule is accepted into NFC by this audit.

## Identifiability review

The execution correctly isolates the next conceptual fork:

1. ordinary dynamics already provides the complete physical probability law, in which case a separate actualization measure may be redundant;
2. actualization contributes additional physical content, in which case it must produce an independently identifiable prediction beyond the supplied dynamics.

A candidate that merely renames normalized transition rates as “actualization probabilities” is not yet a new theory.
