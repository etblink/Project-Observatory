## 3. Primary result

PRIMARY_OUTCOME = C__COMPLETION_CLASS_PARITY_PRESERVED_AT_FROZEN_SCOPE

Accepted-for-review statement:

> Frozen canonical NFC does not source-force a physically meaningful preference for deterministic actualization over genuinely stochastic actualization, or vice versa. The strongest deterministic-looking source structures govern quotient refinement, observable boundary determinacy, canonical representation, persistence, or branch closure rather than unique physical-history actualization. The strongest stochastic-looking source structures are absent from the primitive spine or occur only as later branch/scoped modeling structures and do not supply a source-forced physical measure over histories. A deterministic completion and a genuinely stochastic completion can therefore be added as distinct post-freeze extensions without changing the truth of the frozen structural theorem corpus; the choice between them is new physical content.

## 5. Pro-deterministic reading

### 5.1 Primitive source is nonprobabilistic

Book I states that no primitive probability space is assumed and that probabilistic language, if it later appears, must be earned through certified transfer.

This is not a deterministic actualization axiom. It is an anti-smuggling/license rule. The same source expressly allows probabilistic language to become lawful later.

Disposition:

SOURCE_NONPROBABILISTIC_AT_BASE = YES
GENUINE_STOCHASTIC_COMPLETION_FORBIDDEN = NO
K1_PRIVILEGED = NO

### 5.2 Deterministic Witness Rule / LS-2

Book II contains the Deterministic Witness Rule and collar determinacy. This is the strongest terminology-level candidate for K1 privilege.

It fails P2, P3, and P6.

Book II explicitly states that stabilized local outcomes are observational equivalence classes and that the boundary law does **not** assert determinism of fine-grained interior variation. LS-2 is an observational constraint, not microscopic reconstruction or actual-history selection.

Disposition:

OBSERVABLE_UPDATE_DETERMINACY = YES
FINE_GRAINED_HISTORY_DETERMINISM = NO_ESTABLISHED
ACTUALIZATION_CLASS_PRIVILEGE = NO

### 5.3 Defect monotonicity / temporal preorder

Book I defines an irreversible preorder on history space using cumulative defect weight. This supplies direction/order among histories.

A preorder is not a selector. Multiple histories can have comparable or equal defect values and remain distinct. No theorem promotes minimum defect, maximal persistence, or monotone direction into a law of physical actualization.

Disposition:

HISTORY_ORDERING = YES
UNIQUE_HISTORY_SELECTION = NO
K1_PRIVILEGE = NO

### 5.4 Universal-source uniqueness

Book IV establishes the universal source object and its correct uniqueness notion, but expressly states that it does not certify any specific physical endpoint.

Unique source representation therefore does not imply unique physical future/history.

Disposition:

SOURCE_UNIQUENESS = YES
PHYSICAL_ENDPOINT_UNIQUENESS = NO
K1_PRIVILEGE = NO

### 5.5 Branch projection / canonical endpoint representation

Book V makes branch projection canonical relative to a declared realization functor. Endpoint visibility and legitimacy prevent hidden imports.

This is uniqueness/canonicality **given branch data**. The branch definition permits declared branch-specific hypotheses. Book V does not force one universal realization functor or one history inside a branch.

Disposition:

CANONICALITY_CONDITIONAL_ON_DECLARED_REALIZATION = YES
UNIVERSAL_HISTORY_SELECTOR = NO
K1_PRIVILEGE = NO

### 5.6 SCC recursive stabilization and minimal carrier

SCC provides the strongest selector-like uniqueness machinery: recursive stabilization, MCS, completion, and endpoint identification.

But its own trichotomy explicitly permits `X not spath Y` while `X scap Y`: path-distinct objects described as distinct histories sharing one structural carrier class.

Thus carrier/confluence canonicality does not collapse history multiplicity.

## 6. Pro-stochastic reading

### 6.1 Observational quotient / unresolved multiplicity

Observational equivalence and quotienting leave presentation/microstructure distinctions unresolved.

That does not imply objective probabilities. Ignorance, equivalence, coarse-graining, and physical stochasticity are distinct claims.

### 6.2 Structural entropy / counting

Counting distinguishable classes therefore cannot be promoted to equal probability, maximum entropy, or Born-like weighting without an additional principle.

### 6.3 Multiplicity / branching

Multiple lawful branches and multiple histories are present in the architecture.

Multiplicity alone does not assign weights. It is compatible with deterministic selection, stochastic selection, many-history/no-single-actualization interpretations, or no completed actualization theory.

Disposition:

### 6.4 BIO evolutionary selection dynamics

The BIO branch contains conditional discrete selection dynamics in which population composition updates according to differential replication rates.

This is not a universal stochastic actualization law:

- it is branch-local;
- it is conditional on the biology branch's hereditary/replication structure;
- its population fractions describe composition dynamics;
- it does not assign source-forced probabilities to the universe's alternative admissible histories.

Even if probabilistic or population language is used in a branch, Book V requires branch-specific hypotheses and bridges to remain explicit.

Disposition:

BRANCH_LOCAL_WEIGHTED_DYNAMICS = YES

## 7. PASS / one-way coupling / no-backreaction test

A possible deterministic argument is that one-way coupling or no-backreaction makes evolution effectively feed-forward and therefore deterministic.

This inference is invalid at source scope. Directionality of dependence does not specify whether a forward transition is single-valued or probabilistic. A deterministic transition function and a stochastic Markov kernel can both be one-way/no-backreaction structures.

Therefore PASS-style directionality is completion-class neutral unless an additional theorem supplies single-valuedness or nontrivial transition weights with physical force.

PASS_BREAKS_K1_K2_PARITY = NO

## 8. Representation reversals

### 8.1 Dirac-measure reversal

A deterministic selector can be represented as a Dirac measure. Therefore measure notation cannot establish K2 privilege.

### 8.2 Hidden-variable/enlarged-state reversal

A stochastic observed process can often be represented as deterministic evolution on an enlarged state carrying a random seed/hidden variable. Such an enlargement does not establish K1 privilege because the physical status/distribution of that added variable is extra structure.

### 8.3 Coarse-graining reversal

A deterministic fine-grained process can induce stochastic-looking coarse-grained transitions. Stochastic appearance after quotienting does not establish fundamental K2.

### 8.4 Conclusion

REPRESENTATION_TRICKS_BREAK_PARITY = NO

The frozen source would need an explicit physical statement about which representation carries actualization authority.

## 9. Constructive conservative-extension parity test

The preregistration requires more than search failure. Construct two abstract post-freeze extensions over any frozen NFC admissible-history domain `H` containing at least two physically distinct admissible histories.

### Extension D — deterministic actualization

Add a new post-freeze law

`A_D : H -> H`

with `A_D(H) = h*` for one selected history `h*` (or selected physical-equivalence class), together with the declaration that `h*` is actual.

No frozen NFC theorem needs to be changed: observational quotients, defect ledgers, persistence, source universality, branch legitimacy, SCC carrier structure, and branch-local theorems remain exactly as before. The added law acts only after the frozen admissible domain is formed.

### Extension S — genuinely stochastic actualization

Add instead a new post-freeze normalized measure/kernel

`mu_S` on `H`

with nonzero weight on at least two physically distinct admissible histories and a new physical rule interpreting those weights as actualization probabilities/propensities.

Again, no frozen structural theorem needs to be changed. Book I's anti-smuggling discipline says probability is not primitive; it does not prohibit a later separately licensed probabilistic structure. Book V already supplies the governance pattern for explicit new downstream hypotheses/bridges.

### Comparison

Both D and S preserve the entire frozen theorem truth set while adding mutually inequivalent physical actualization content.

The source does not derive either added law.

## 11. Outcome discrimination

### Outcome A — rejected

No frozen source principle privileges deterministic history actualization. Deterministic terminology and uniqueness theorems concern narrower structural objects and explicitly fail scope/physical-force tests.

### Outcome B — rejected

No frozen source principle privileges genuinely stochastic history actualization. Multiplicity, quotienting, entropy, and branch-local weighted dynamics do not produce a source-forced physical measure over histories.

### Outcome C — selected

A constructive deterministic/stochastic extension pair preserves frozen theorem truth while differing in added physical actualization content. No source theorem discriminates between them at the audited scope.

### Outcome D — rejected as primary

Branch-local asymmetries exist, most notably BIO selection dynamics and other conditional branch dynamics, but they do not obstruct the universal conservative-extension parity construction. They are secondary observations under C.

### Outcome E — rejected

The source authority is sufficient for the bounded negative result and constructive independence argument.

### Outcome F — rejected

## 12. Scientific consequence

The prior completion-class result is strengthened:

`COMPLETION_CLASS_NONUNIQUENESS`

becomes

`COMPLETION_CLASS_PARITY_NONFORCING_AT_FROZEN_SOURCE_SCOPE`.

The open object is therefore two-layered:

1. NFC lacks a source-forced actualization law over admissible histories at the audited scope.
2. NFC also lacks a source-forced principle deciding whether any future actualization completion should be deterministic or genuinely stochastic.

This does not prove that nature is neutral between those options. It proves only that frozen NFC is.

## 13. Nonclaims

This audit does not establish:

- that deterministic completion is physically correct;
- that stochastic completion is physically correct;
- that probabilities cannot emerge in NFC;
- that deterministic branch dynamics are invalid;
- that stochastic branch dynamics are fundamental;
- that every admissible history must be actual;
- that a future NFC extension cannot derive a parity breaker after adding genuinely new premises;
- that determinism and stochasticity are mathematically disjoint under all representations.

## 14. Routing recommendation

Independent acceptance should test especially:

1. whether the D/S conservative-extension pair genuinely preserves frozen theorem truth;
2. whether Book I's nonprobabilistic primitive stance was correctly treated as nonexclusive rather than deterministic;
3. whether any deterministic-looking canonical theorem was mistakenly scoped too narrowly;
4. whether BIO or another branch contains a source-lift theorem that would defeat universal parity;
5. whether Outcome C is stronger than justified by the source.

If accepted, the next highest-information question is not another search for hidden determinism/stochasticity. It is to classify **what kind of new empirical or theoretical evidence could break the parity** without circularly assuming one completion class.
