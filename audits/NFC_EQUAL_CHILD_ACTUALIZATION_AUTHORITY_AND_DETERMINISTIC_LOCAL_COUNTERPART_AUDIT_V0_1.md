## Q1 — Equal-child authority

### E1/E2: observational quotient, behavioral congruence, structural invariance

FAIL AS PHYSICAL-AUTHORITY BRIDGE.

Book I establishes that canonical distinctions are observationally generated, that raw-label inverse selection is illicit, and that structural invariance preserves structure under isomorphism. These statements control representation dependence and admissibility. They do not say that two **distinct** quotient-visible child classes have equal physical propensity.

The crucial distinction is:

- observational equivalence removes distinctions;
- lawful siblings in a refinement are distinct quotient classes;
- structural invariance applies to structurally equivalent presentations;
- equal actualization weight is an additional physical claim about distinct alternatives.

No theorem bridges the last step.

### E3: Binary Rigidity

FAIL AS GENERAL AUTHORITY.

Binary Rigidity concerns stable compression fibers under B-stable/toggle-admissible conditions and the WL-1 single-edge merge regime. It does not prove that every Book-I continuation node has exactly two children, and even a two-child node does not by itself imply physical probability `1/2`.

If a future theorem lifted binary symmetry to the relevant continuation domain, equal weighting would remain a separate authority question unless the theorem also supplied the probability bridge.

### E4: PASS

FAIL.

PASS preserves declared backbone invariants under admissible continuation. It constrains lawful evolution and collapse. It contains no probability or physical-indifference clause over alternative lawful children.

### E5: source canonicity / source descent

FAIL.

Canonical source status identifies the lawful origin and comparison structure of downstream objects. By Book VII, source-side canonicality cannot self-promote to downstream physical interpretation without a separate transfer theorem. No equal-weight transfer theorem exists.

### E6: defect/entropy structure

FAIL AS AUTHORITY; SUCCESS AS NATURAL COORDINATE.

Book I canonically defines child count by

`k(C)=delta(C)+1`.

This makes

`P(C'|C)=1/k(C)`

mathematically parameter-free and locally normalized if equal-child indifference is added. But Book I explicitly denies primitive probabilistic interpretation at this stage and treats defect bookkeeping as combinatorial before later licensing.

Thus the defect ledger gives a natural coordinate for a probability rule, not physical authority for the rule.

### E7: branch-local normalized/selection dynamics

FAIL TO LIFT.

BIO contains conditional discrete selection dynamics and SPEC contains certified probe-induced transition ledgers. Those are branch-scoped constructions with declared target regimes and dependencies. No source theorem promotes them into a universal history-actualization law.

Therefore the same frozen source is compatible with equal weighting and unequal quotient-visible weighting.

`EQUAL_WEIGHT_SOURCE_FORCED = NO`

## Q2 — Deterministic local counterpart

### D1: minimum one-step defect

NOT SOURCE-FORCED.

A rule choosing the child with smallest subsequent `delta` is a legitimate candidate generator if the minimum is unique. But frozen NFC contains no theorem that physical actualization must minimize defect. Moreover ties are lawful.

A two-child refinement witness can have

`delta(C1)=delta(C2)=0`

while `C1 != C2` as quotient classes. Both are unique-continuation children thereafter, so local defect provides no tie-break.

### D2: minimum cumulative defect over a window

NOT SOURCE-FORCED AND WINDOW-DEPENDENT.

This is a mathematically meaningful candidate but requires a horizon/window and an authority principle `actual history minimizes cumulative defect`. Neither is source-forced. Equal minima can also occur.

### D3: temporal preorder

FAIL AS SELECTOR.

The defect preorder orders histories by cumulative defect. An order is not a choice function. Partial/equal ranking remains possible, and no source theorem promotes minimal elements to actual histories.

### D4: SCC minimal carrier / canonicality

FAIL AS HISTORY SELECTOR.

SCC can canonicalize carriers while retaining distinct histories. Its branch trichotomy explicitly permits confluent distinctness: distinct histories can share the same structural carrier class. Therefore carrier minimality/canonicality cannot serve as a total actual-history selector.

### D5: Deterministic Witness Rule

FAIL AS ACTUALIZER.

Book II's DW says that, for each specified admissible extension, the induced partition update is uniquely determined by the current WL color/collar class. It is determinism of the **observable partition update conditional on the extension**, not a theorem selecting which admissible extension or which lawful physical continuation occurs.

Book II separately states that its boundary law does not assert determinism of fine-grained interior variation.

### D6: unique continuation (`delta=0`)

PASS ONLY AS TRIVIAL SUBREGIME.

When `delta(C)=0`, Book I proves there is exactly one descendant class at the next stage. In that local subregime there is no multiplicity to actualize: every supported actualization law concentrated on lawful children must assign the sole child probability one.

This is a source-forced **unique-continuation fact**, but not a nontrivial actualization theorem. It does not resolve nodes with `delta>0`.

### D7: quotient-visible canonical ordering

NO TOTAL SOURCE RULE IDENTIFIED.

Frozen NFC contains many canonical invariants and structural orderings, but no branch-independent theorem defines a strict total order on every lawful sibling set and then grants the least/greatest element physical actualization authority. Raw token/label lexicography would violate anti-smuggling.

## Anti-deterministic reversal

A lawful parent `C` may split into distinct quotient classes `C1` and `C2` with identical immediate child count and identical local defect burden. Let both have unique descendants thereafter. Then:

- both are lawful;
- neither can be distinguished by raw-label rules;
- `delta(C1)=delta(C2)=0`;
- cumulative defect can tie over every finite future horizon if the two branches remain structurally parallel;
- SCC-style carrier canonicality need not collapse history distinctness.

Any deterministic choice between them therefore requires either a further quotient-visible discriminator plus a new extremization authority principle, or an illicit hidden tie-break.

`DETERMINISTIC_LOCAL_SELECTOR_SOURCE_FORCED = NO`

## Why this does not break deterministic/stochastic source parity

The asymmetry is one of construction burden:

- stochastic: one universal local premise, `equal lawful children receive equal physical weight`, yields a normalized projective family;
- deterministic: a nontrivial total local rule requires at least a ranking/discriminator and a physical extremization authority, plus a tie policy or theorem excluding ties.

Fewer formal moving parts do not constitute evidence that stochastic actualization is physically true.
