## 3. Frozen setup

Let Z be a lawful post-interaction quotient-visible state/record produced by an admissible observation-like probe transition.

Let H(Z) be the nonempty class of lawful post-Z continuation histories permitted by frozen NFC structure.

Frozen NFC currently supplies neither:

- a theorem forcing |H(Z)| = 1 in general; nor
- a source-forced normalized physical measure on H(Z).

The bridge problem is to identify the weakest extra schema that would convert Z into actualization force while preserving NFC's anti-inversion and no-smuggling constraints.

## 4. Candidate bridge classes

B1 — DETERMINISTIC_UNIQUENESS
A theorem/axiom schema forces exactly one lawful continuation h*(Z) in H(Z), or supplies a representation-invariant selection functional S(Z)=h*(Z).

B2 — STOCHASTIC_KERNEL
A theorem/axiom schema supplies a normalized physical kernel K(.|Z) on H(Z), with nontrivial weights permitted and with explicit physical interpretation.

B3 — TWO_STAGE_RECORD_THEN_SELECTION
Observation first creates Z; a second declared selector law A_Z then acts only on H(Z). This can instantiate B1 or B2 but makes provenance separation explicit.

B4 — ABSTRACT_ACTUALIZATION_INTERFACE
A single abstract schema A assigns to each Z a normalized measure A_Z on H(Z), with deterministic cases represented as Dirac measures. This is a common formal interface, not necessarily one physical completion class.

B5 — CONSTRAINT_COMPLETION
Existing NFC constraints plus one extra post-interaction constraint reduce H(Z) to one history or to canonically weighted classes.

B6 — NO_MINIMAL_NEUTRAL_BRIDGE
Any bridge strong enough to actualize must already choose substantive deterministic or stochastic physics; no physically neutral common bridge exists beyond a bookkeeping interface.

## 5. Qualification criteria

A bridge candidate must satisfy:

Q1 POST_INTERACTION_DOMAIN — acts only on lawful quotient-visible post-interaction state Z and H(Z).
Q2 NO_PRESTATE_INVERSION — never selects by recovering information erased by the pre-interaction quotient.
Q3 HISTORY_FORCE — returns one actual continuation or a normalized physical weighting over continuations.
Q4 REPRESENTATION_INVARIANCE — equivalent presentations of Z/H(Z) produce equivalent actualization content.
Q5 NORMALIZATION_OR_UNIQUENESS — deterministic uniqueness or stochastic normalization is explicit.
Q6 PROVENANCE — any extra physical assumption is named rather than hidden in semantics or bridge notation.
Q7 NONCIRCULARITY — the outcome/history is not assumed in the rule definition.
Q8 FAILURE_EXPOSURE — conditions for nonexistence/nonuniqueness/inconsistency are explicit.
Q9 COMPOSITIONALITY — sequential observation events can be composed without contradictory actualization assignments.
Q10 SOURCE_COMPATIBILITY — the bridge does not contradict frozen quotient/persistence/branch-legitimacy results.

## 6. Minimality tests

For each candidate determine:

- whether removing any substantive component destroys history-force;
- whether it merely renames deterministic/stochastic completion;
- whether it introduces hidden auxiliary freedom;
- whether it creates selector regress;
- whether deterministic and genuinely stochastic cases remain physically inequivalent despite any common notation.

## 7. Mandatory reversals

### Common-interface reversal
Try to show that a normalized actualization measure A_Z on H(Z) is the weakest common formal bridge, with deterministic actualization recovered as a Dirac case.

Then test whether this is scientifically substantive or only notation.

### No-neutral-bridge reversal
Try to show that the moment A_Z is given physical force, one must decide whether nontrivial weights are physically meaningful; therefore the common interface cannot itself solve the physical completion problem.

### Existing-NFC reversal
Test whether any frozen NFC object already supplies enough additional structure after Z to instantiate B1/B2 without new physics.

## 8. Outcome taxonomy

A__COMMON_MINIMAL_ACTUALIZATION_BRIDGE_IS_PHYSICALLY_SUBSTANTIVE
One weakest bridge schema covers both deterministic and stochastic completions without hiding a substantive physical choice.

B__COMMON_FORMAL_INTERFACE_EXISTS__PHYSICAL_COMPLETION_REMAINS_BIFURCATED
A common mathematical bridge interface exists, but deterministic and genuinely stochastic cases remain physically inequivalent and require additional substantive choice.

C__DETERMINISTIC_MINIMAL_BRIDGE_PRIVILEGED
NFC-compatible minimality uniquely favors deterministic continuation.

D__STOCHASTIC_MINIMAL_BRIDGE_PRIVILEGED
NFC-compatible minimality uniquely favors a stochastic kernel.

E__TWO_STAGE_ARCHITECTURE_REQUIRED
Observation-record creation and actualization must remain separate stages for consistency/provenance reasons.

F__NO_COHERENT_OBSERVATION_LINKED_BRIDGE
Anti-inversion or other frozen constraints prevent any coherent bridge architecture.

G__UNDERDETERMINED
The bounded source does not permit a clean result.

H__REPAIR_REQUIRED
A source/protocol inconsistency prevents adjudication.

## 9. Decision rule

Do not mistake a common probability-measure notation for a common physical law. Outcome A requires physical, not merely formal, neutrality.

Outcome B is justified if the measure-valued interface is the weakest common formal contract but nontrivial stochastic weights introduce physical commitments absent from deterministic Dirac selection.

Outcome E requires showing that a one-stage rule necessarily obscures provenance or violates anti-inversion; mere conceptual convenience is insufficient.

## 10. Stop rule

After execution and independent acceptance, stop before proposing numerical weights, a concrete deterministic selector, quantum measurement dynamics, or any empirical prediction.
