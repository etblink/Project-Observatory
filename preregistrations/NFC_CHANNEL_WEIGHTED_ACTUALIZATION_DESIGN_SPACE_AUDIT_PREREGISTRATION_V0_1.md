## Question

After rejection of universal raw equal-child weighting at validated spectroscopy scope, does frozen NFC or its frozen branches supply any noncircular, quotient-visible channel quantity that can serve as a prospective nonnegative weight `W(C -> C_i)` in

`P(C_i|C) = W(C->C_i) / sum_j W(C->C_j)`

without defining `W` from observed branching frequencies or silently importing an external quantum probability rule?

## Candidate weight classes

W1 structural multiplicity / degeneracy
W2 defect / continuation burden
W3 transport / coercive cost
W4 spectral position / spectral margin
W5 probe-response magnitude / transition-ledger quantity
W6 symmetry / representation factor
W7 branch-local generator or transition-strength object
W8 explicitly new physical weight primitive
W9 externally derived dynamical quantity with separately proved bridge
W10 no justified noncircular weight

## Acceptance gates

A candidate weight must be:
- child-specific and prospectively computable;
- quotient-visible / representation-invariant at declared scope;
- nonnegative with a clear zero meaning;
- fixed independently of outcome frequencies used to test it;
- compatible with normalization;
- not merely a restatement of channel identity;
- accompanied by a physical-authority argument or explicitly labeled new physics;
- failure-exposed on at least one realizable target.
