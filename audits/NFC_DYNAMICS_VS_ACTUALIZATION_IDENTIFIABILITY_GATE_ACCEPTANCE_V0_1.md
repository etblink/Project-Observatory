## Scope review

PASS WITH IMPORTANT QUALIFICATION.

The accepted result is conditional on the phrase **complete declared physical transition kernel**. The audit does not establish that frozen NFC already supplies such a kernel. It establishes that, once the complete observable transition statistics are fixed, a second layer that merely samples from those same statistics is empirically redundant.

Therefore the result must not be cited as:

- `NFC proves no actualization exists`, or
- `all actualization theories are unscientific`, or
- `dynamics is already complete in frozen NFC`.

None of those claims was proved.

## Formal identifiability review

PASS.

If two models generate identical probability distributions for every prospectively fixed observable and intervention, then no statistical experiment on those observables can distinguish them. A separately named selector that simply samples according to the dynamics-supplied kernel is therefore non-identifiable at that scope.

This is a genuine identifiability result, not an ontology result.

## Pro-actualization reversal review

PASS.

The execution correctly leaves open actualization theories that alter:

- one-step frequencies;
- multi-step correlations;
- intervention response;
- allowed histories;
- separately observable state variables; or
- branch/scope dependence.

Any such theory can be scientifically distinct because it changes at least one prediction beyond the dynamics-only model.

No frozen NFC object satisfying this requirement was identified.

## Frozen-source review

PASS.

Book I supplies alternatives/quotients/continuation multiplicity without primitive probability or physical interpretation. Book II supplies scoped observable determinacy without microscopic actualization. Book III supplies transport/coercive comparison rather than a probability law. Books IV-VII supply source descent, branch legitimacy, interfaces, and governance rather than a second outcome kernel.

The execution correctly avoids converting governance or canonicity into physical probability authority.

## Ten-branch review

PASS AT AUDITED ROLE SCOPE.

The complete branch census contains examples of branch-specific dynamics and selection language, most notably BIO, but no second universal actualization layer whose observable predictions remain distinct after conditioning on the complete declared dynamics.

BIO is an especially useful control because its conditional population update already carries the probability-bearing/dynamical content. Re-labeling the same normalized update as 'actualization' adds no observable prediction.

SPEC likewise supplies a transition/probe architecture but no frozen transition-amplitude probability law and no additional actualizer.

## ELCAK adverse-control review

PASS.

Raw ELCAK was scientifically meaningful precisely because it *changed* the predicted spectroscopy kernel to equal-child weights and was then contradicted by known nonuniform branching fractions. That case confirms the audit's identifiability criterion: an actualization proposal becomes physical when it changes a prediction.

A weighted successor that merely copies externally supplied dynamical branching ratios would not regain independent empirical content.

## Residual frontier

The accepted frontier is now bifurcated:

1. `TRANSITION_KERNEL_DERIVATION`: Can NFC or its branches derive physically calibrated transition probabilities from source/branch structure?
2. `RESIDUAL_ACTUALIZATION_ONTOLOGY`: After the kernel is fixed, is there any additional theory with a distinct observable consequence?

Only the first is presently an active empirical/mathematical frontier. The second remains open but is not yet a separate scientific law at audited scope.
