## Dynamics-versus-actualization objection

CONSIDERED AND SCOPE-LIMITED.

The objection is correct that atomic branching ratios reflect physical transition dynamics, not a theorem that observation itself creates the decay. But raw ELCAK was proposed precisely as a probability law over lawful post-interaction/continuation children using sibling count alone. Once an independently specified physical domain supplies nonuniform channel propensities, a universal count-only law cannot remain valid on that domain.

The correct scientific response is not to redefine the children after seeing the data. It is to conclude that any viable extension covering ordinary spectroscopy must incorporate channel-sensitive dynamical structure before or within the actualization weighting law.

This acceptance therefore does **not** establish that “observation is not involved in actualization.” It establishes the narrower and defensible result:
