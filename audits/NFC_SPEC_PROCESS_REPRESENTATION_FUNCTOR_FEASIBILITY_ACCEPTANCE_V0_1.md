## Book-V review

PASS AND SCIENTIFICALLY IMPORTANT.

## Free-representation review

PASS WITH SCOPE GUARDRAIL.

After an algebraic target choice such as a semiring/ring/field `k`, the certified episode/path category admits the usual free `k`-linear enrichment and associated regular representations. The canonicity is relative to that target choice and its universal property.

This is a mathematical consequence of the reconstructed categorical structure, not a claim that frozen NFC selects `k`, a Hilbert space, or physical amplitudes.

The ledger may be retained as an additive grading/label; it must not silently become a coefficient.
