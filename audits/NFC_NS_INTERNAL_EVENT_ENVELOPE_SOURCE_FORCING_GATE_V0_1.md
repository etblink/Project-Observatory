## Evidence adjudication

### F1 Book-I finiteness
Book I works with finite relational objects and requires finite output control for each admissible process. These conditions guarantee finite defect arithmetic for every declared finite step. They do not supply one global bound on the number of distinct internal defect-event types or on the number of certified interior events across an arbitrarily long/enlarging branch tail.

Finite valence is likewise pointwise. It prevents infinite local branching at one licensed configuration; it does not imply a uniform bound on the number of interior sites or defect events exposed by later enlargements.

### F2 stabilization
Finite stabilization closes a fixed admissible refinement program once its quotient stops splitting. The NS enlargement/transport architecture ranges over successive contexts/windows. Current canon does not prove that every later internal enlargement event is governed by one already-stabilized finite internal event state. Therefore stabilization cannot be repurposed as a uniform N_int theorem without an additional bridge.

### F3 LS-2 / K0
LS-2 and the finite collar alphabet control stabilized local outcomes through bounded collar information. They explicitly do not reconstruct microscopic interior structure. Thus they do not force a finite uniform internal event alphabet or multiplicity ceiling.

### F4 Book-III E_n
E_n is additive over certified interior-site defect increments during U_n -> U_{n+1}. The definition permits any finite number of such events at an individual step; it contains no branch-uniform cardinality cap.

### F5 UWB
UWB bounds late window-boundary size and thereby yields a finite collar-labelled window-configuration space. It does not bound interior volume or the number/type multiplicity of interior defect events.

### F6 Phase-A / K0=7
These give strong finite collar structure and quantitative boundary/mixing information but no theorem making all internal defect generation a uniformly finite boundary-controlled process.

## Countermodel shape
Consider an admissible family of finite relational enlargement steps with:
- fixed/bounded quotient-visible collar transition data satisfying LS-2 and the UWB boundary restriction;
- finite valence and finite output at every step;
- an increasing finite number of pairwise admissible interior defect-producing motifs or quotient-splitting events whose effects remain internal and do not alter the stabilized collar transition.

Every individual step remains finite and compliant with the stated local finiteness rules, while E_n and/or the number of internal event classes/multiplicities can grow without one tail-uniform bound. Nothing in the frozen source rules identified above excludes this shape.

Therefore per-step finiteness does not imply the selected uniform event envelope.
