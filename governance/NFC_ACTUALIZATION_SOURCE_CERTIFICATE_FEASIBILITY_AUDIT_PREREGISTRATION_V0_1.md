## Certificate criterion

A candidate `S_A` qualifies only if it establishes source-certified content that can noncircularly bear on actualization among multiple lawful histories. It must do more than:

- define or restrict admissibility;
- order histories without choosing or weighting them;
- prove persistence, stability, convergence, capacity, or bounded transport;
- identify a canonical quotient, carrier, completion, endpoint representation, or equivalence class;
- supply branch-specific evolution after additional branch data are declared;
- state that one history is actual, preferred, selected, probable, or extremal without deriving the relevant force.

A candidate may qualify if it proves, from frozen source primitives alone, a nontrivial uniqueness, weighting, variational, fixed-point, inevitability, symmetry-breaking, or other theorem whose content narrows actualization and does not assume the selector in its hypotheses.

## Mandatory screens

1. Spine primitive/combinatorial screen: admissible processes, maximal admissible class, defect ledger, entropy, temporal preorder, boundary law, PASS, transport/coercivity, universal source, branch legitimacy, continuum interfaces, governance.
2. Ten-branch exhaustive screen: BIO, CRYST, GR, LING, NS, RH, SCC, SM, SPEC, YM.
3. Cross-branch recurrence screen: determine whether a common theorem form recurring independently across branches can be lifted sourceward without importing branch-specific hypotheses.
4. Conservative-completion test: where possible, construct two distinct actualization completions preserving the same candidate source certificate. If both remain lawful, that certificate does not determine actualization.
5. Circularity test: reject any candidate whose force depends on an assumed history, selector, measure, target dynamics, initial condition, realization functor, or physical interpretation that is not source-forced.
6. Pro-certificate reversal: construct the strongest possible derivation for `S_A` before rejecting it.
7. Anti-certificate reversal: attempt to preserve all frozen source truths while varying actualization.
