## Scope

This is a closed-source theorem-authority audit. It does not claim logical impossibility for every conceivable future formalization. It establishes nonderivability at the audited frozen scope: the frozen source truths admit multiple distinct actualization completions, and none of the ten branch books supplies a branch result that can be lifted sourceward without importing branch-specific structure.

## Required object

The target object is `S_A`: source-certified content, derived without an actualization postulate, that narrows which lawful history is physically actual (or supplies a forced weighting over lawful histories) strongly enough to support a later actualization transfer theorem `T_A`.

### Book I

### Book II

### Book III

The Unified Coercive-Transport Inequality and related persistence/transport results constrain lawful transfer and bound drift. They are inequalities and accounting laws; no theorem promotes minimization, saturation, or extremization of their quantities into a physical actualization principle.

### Book IV

Universal-source uniqueness/factorization supplies source-side canonicality. It selects a canonical source object, not an actual history generated from that source.

### Book V

Branch legitimacy certifies lawful descent, visibility, declared hypotheses, and no hidden supplementation. It permits multiple honestly declared branch/model structures and therefore cannot choose among actualization completions.

### Book VI

Continuum interfaces license faithful target notation/limits. They transfer already-certified content and do not create source-side actualization force.

### Book VII

Governance explicitly blocks self-promotion. Source-side canonicality does not automatically become physical endpoint identification. Governance provides the schema for a future `T_A`; it does not supply `S_A`.

## Strongest pro-certificate constructions

### Candidate 1: defect/entropy extremization

Attempt: combine irreversible defect order, entropy monotonicity and persistence selection into a least-defect or least-entropy actualization law.

Failure: the frozen theorems provide order/monotonicity but no theorem saying physical history must minimize, maximize, saturate, or sample according to these quantities. Adding that step is exactly additional actualization content.

### Candidate 2: persistence + coercivity + fixed-point inevitability

Attempt: argue that repeated lawful transport forces convergence to one continuation, yielding actualization by inevitability.

Failure: convergence/stability results control observables or carriers under declared hypotheses; they do not establish unique global history. SCC supplies a direct multiplicity witness after recursive stabilization.

### Candidate 3: universal source + canonical branch descent

Attempt: use uniqueness of `U` and canonical projections to infer unique physical realization/history.

Failure: uniqueness of source or representation does not imply uniqueness of descendant realization or actual history. Book V explicitly allows declared branch-specific hypotheses; Book VII blocks source-to-endpoint self-promotion.

### Candidate 4: cross-branch recurrence

Attempt: treat repeated selection-like forms across BIO, SCC, CRYST, RH, YM/SM, NS/GR and SPEC as evidence of a common source selector.

Failure: the repeated pattern is weaker and identifiable: admissibility filtering, stability, minimal representation, target evolution, exclusion, or controlled response. The branch-specific hypotheses differ, and stripping them away leaves the spine-level constraint machinery already tested above. Recurrence therefore supports a common **constraint grammar**, not an actualization theorem.

## Conservative-completion / independence argument

Let `N` be the frozen source theory and `H(Z)` the set of histories remaining lawful after all source-level admissibility, quotient, persistence, transport and governance constraints. The earlier accepted source-forcing audit established that more than one actualization completion can be added while preserving the same frozen source truths.

For any state/regime `Z` with at least two source-lawful histories `h1,h2 in H(Z)`, consider two openly declared completions:

- `M1 = N + A_Z(h1)=1` with an otherwise lawful deterministic actualization rule selecting `h1`;
- `M2 = N + A_Z(h2)=1` with an otherwise lawful deterministic actualization rule selecting `h2`.

Alternatively, assign two distinct normalized measures `mu1 != mu2` supported on `H(Z)` while leaving every theorem of `N` unchanged.

Because the frozen source contains no theorem preferring `h1` to `h2`, nor `mu1` to `mu2`, both are conservative with respect to the audited source truths so long as they respect existing admissibility constraints. The ten branch screens do not collapse this freedom: their strongest selector-like structures require branch-specific target data or select representations/classes rather than universal physical histories.

Therefore no proposition derivable from the frozen source alone can function as the required `S_A` while distinguishing these completions. If it did, `M1` and `M2` could not both preserve the same source theory.

This is the constructive basis for Outcome D; it is not a keyword-search failure.

## Circularity result

Every route that succeeds in choosing or weighting actual histories must add at least one premise not forced by the frozen source: e.g. an extremal principle, stochastic measure/kernel, initial/boundary actualization condition, symmetry-breaking rule, target-specific dynamical law promoted beyond branch scope, or another physical criterion with equivalent force.

Calling that premise a 'derivation rule' rather than a postulate does not change its status unless its force is independently proved from source premises.

## Meaning of `NEW_PHYSICAL_CONTENT_REQUIRED`

This phrase is deliberately scoped. It means: **relative to frozen canon `ed3047c2...`, an actualization-relevant source certificate cannot be obtained without adding scientific content that is not already theorem-forced by that source.**

It does not mean the new content must be arbitrary, metaphysical, or empirically inaccessible. It could be a new physical postulate, a new empirically constrained law, a new source axiom, or a separately justified principle later shown to have physical authority. It also does not preclude a future expanded NFC theory from deriving the selector after such content is admitted.

## Secondary finding: branch grammar pattern

Across all ten branches, a recurring architecture is visible: source-descended admissible arena + branch-specific grammar/observable family + constraints/closure + target endpoint. This is a scientifically interesting structural recurrence, but it is not used as proof of PGH or of actualization. It is recorded only as a future crosswalk target.
