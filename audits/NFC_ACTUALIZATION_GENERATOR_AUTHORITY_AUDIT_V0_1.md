## Primary outcome

`C__INTERNAL_QUANTITIES_CONSTRAIN_WITHOUT_ACTUALIZATION_AUTHORITY`

## Executive finding

Frozen NFC contains several quantities and structures that strongly constrain lawful histories, observable distinctions, local outcomes, transport, persistence, response transitions, branch identity, and carrier minimality. None of the audited candidates is source-authorized to determine which lawful continuation is physically actual or to assign source-forced physical weights over lawful continuations.

This is not a search-failure result. The decisive evidence is constructive and scope-positive: the strongest candidates are explicitly typed as bookkeeping, boundary control, transport/stability, quotient/classification, branch-local transition, or structural carrier machinery, and the frozen corpus permits multiplicity after those quantities have done their canonical work.

## Candidate-family adjudication

### 1. Book-I continuation defect and cumulative defect ledger

The continuation defect and cumulative ledger measure branching burden along survivor chains. The ledger is additive and induces an irreversible preorder on histories by comparing cumulative defect.

Authority verdict: **NO**.

Reason: the source uses Delta to record and order continuation burden. It does not state that lower-Delta histories are physically actual, more probable, or uniquely preferred. Any transformation such as `exp(-Delta)` would be an added completion law, not a theorem consequence.

The temporal preorder therefore supplies ordered structure without selection force.

### 2. Structural entropy / quotient multiplicity

Structural entropy `S = log|Sigma_infty|` measures the number of admissible distinctions and is monotone under the declared refinement conditions.

Authority verdict: **NO**.

Reason: its canonical role is distinguishability bookkeeping. No frozen theorem identifies lower entropy, higher entropy, maximal entropy production, or any other entropy functional as an actualization principle. The source explicitly withholds thermodynamic/probabilistic interpretation at this level.

### 3. Book-II boundary/collar laws and deterministic witness machinery

Book II proves local observable control and capacity bounds. Under LS-2, stabilized local observable outcome is controlled by stabilized collar type. The deterministic witness condition concerns partition updates and observational classes.

Authority verdict: **NO**.

Reason: Book II explicitly distinguishes observable-class determinacy from microscopic interior determinism. Boundary/collar capacity excludes or constrains local regimes; it does not choose one physical history among lawful histories.

### 4. Book-III transport/coercivity/interface quantities

The Unified Coercive-Transport Inequality constrains interface-relative stability by transported bulk, internal defect, and boundary defect. Transfer coercivity bounds uncontrolled observational drift.

Authority verdict: **NO**.

Reason: these are certified balance and transport-control relations. They can constrain which continuations remain lawful or stable, but no theorem states that the physically actual continuation minimizes the coercive bound, minimizes interface burden, maximizes transport factor, or otherwise extremizes these quantities.

### 5. SPEC probe-response and transition-ledger machinery

SPEC gives the strongest observation-linked positive mechanism. A lawful probe episode can induce quotient-visible `X -> X'` change with a certified before/after response ledger, and transition certification is conditionally discharged.

Authority verdict: **NO universal actualization authority**.

Reason: the transition ledger certifies a lawful response transition. It does not determine which later continuation from the post-probe state is physically actual. SPEC itself treats its probe episodes as controlled admissible comparisons, not yet ordinary external measurement events.

### 6. SCC recursive stabilization, CapRec, path/cap equivalence, MCS, completion, and terminal structure

SCC provides some of the strongest uniqueness/minimality/canonicality language in the corpus.

Authority verdict: **NO**.

Reason: SCC's own trichotomy explicitly permits confluent distinctness: recursively stabilized objects can correspond to distinct histories while sharing the same structural carrier class. Thus carrier canonicity and recursive closure survive history multiplicity. MCS and branchwise completion therefore cannot be promoted into actual-history selection without an additional theorem.

### 7. Branch-local deterministic/variational/selection-like mechanisms

BIO selection dynamics, YM/GR dynamical or variational structures, NS continuation/dynamical structures, and related branch-local machinery were treated as candidate analogues.

Authority verdict: **BRANCH_LOCAL_OR_CONDITIONAL_ONLY**.

Reason: their force depends on declared branch data, realization structure, initial conditions, kernels, Hamiltonians, or other branch-specific hypotheses. No frozen source theorem lifts any one branch-local rule into a universal actualization law over NFC histories.

## Mandatory adversarial tests

### Normalization fallacy

FAILS as a route to authority.

Multiple NFC quantities can be converted mathematically into normalized weights. The frozen source does not license those conversions as physical probabilities. Mathematical normalizability is therefore insufficient.

### Extremum fallacy

FAILS as a route to authority.

Minimal carrier, maximal admissible class, bounded defect, stabilized quotient, and other extrema/canonical objects are typed to structural or representational roles. None is source-linked to physical actualization.

### Ordering-vs-selection

PASS for anti-authority conclusion.

Delta defines a history preorder, but the source provides no theorem mapping that preorder to physical selection.

### Branch-to-source lifting

PASS for anti-authority conclusion.

No audited branch-local dynamics acquire universal source authority.

### Observation-backaction

PASS for anti-authority conclusion.

Observation-like probe interaction can alter quotient-visible state, but this does not itself choose the physically actual post-probe continuation.

### Conservative completion test

PASS.

Take any lawful post-observation state Z whose continuation set H(Z) contains at least two histories not excluded by the frozen structural machinery. Two post-freeze completions can be attached without changing the truth of the audited frozen theorems:

1. deterministic completion `A_Z = delta_{h_*}` for one lawful history `h_*`;
2. genuinely stochastic completion `A_Z` with nontrivial normalized support on at least two lawful histories.

The defect ledger, structural entropy, collar law, coercive bounds, transition ledgers, SCC carrier structure, and branch-legitimacy theorems retain their stated roles in both completions, provided the completion respects their admissibility constraints. Therefore those quantities do not themselves source-force the form or value of `A_Z`.

This construction establishes nonforcing with respect to frozen theorem truth; it does not declare either completion future canonical NFC.

## Strongest pro-authority chain

The strongest available chain is:

`admissible internal test -> quotient-visible probe response -> lawful X -> X' transition -> persistence/transport accounting -> SCC/branch structural stabilization -> lawful continuation set H(Z)`

The chain terminates before:

`H(Z) -> A_Z`.

No audited theorem supplies the final arrow.

## Strongest anti-authority result

The decisive obstruction is not merely missing wording. It is the coexistence of:

- explicit history ordering without physical-selection semantics;
- observable/class determinacy without microscopic determinism;
- transport/stability bounds without an extremal actualization theorem;
- probe-induced transitions without post-transition actualization force;
- SCC carrier canonicality with explicit distinct-history multiplicity;
- branch-local selection/dynamics without source-level lifting.

Together these positively delimit the current authority of the internal quantities.

## Sharpened frontier

The missing object is no longer merely a generic selector. It is an **authority bridge** connecting lawful NFC-internal structure to the physical actualization interface:

`Q_NFC(Z,h)  --[missing authority theorem]-->  A_Z(h)`.

The source already supplies many possible `Q_NFC`. What is absent is a theorem explaining why any one of them should have physical actualization force.
