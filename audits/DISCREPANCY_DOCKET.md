## V0.4 note

`OBS-D005` is operationally important because branch count alone is misleading. The accepted scientific record is distributed across divergent refs. Deleting acceptance refs merely because a later snapshot exists can make otherwise independent histories unreachable.

The branch-retention audit therefore treats accepted adjudication refs as provenance anchors, while allowing redundant preregistration/execution refs to be pruned where the acceptance commit retains them in ancestry.

`OBS-D004` remains an Observatory finding only. More generally, accepted Observatory findings remain external analytical findings unless and until a separately governed source-project operation adopts or adjudicates them.
