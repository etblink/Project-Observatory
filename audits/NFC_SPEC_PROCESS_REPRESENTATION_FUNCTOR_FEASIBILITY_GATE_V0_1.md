## 1. Source-level categorical structure

Book I supplies admissible extension processes with closure under **typed composition**, structural invariance, base-signature accountability, and finite-output control. It does not require every process to be an endomorphism of one fixed object. Therefore the generic source structure is typed and category-like, not a single transformation monoid.

The formal identity extension may serve as a composition unit but is explicitly not automatically a licensed observational test. Hence the representation domain must distinguish categorical identities from physical/probe episodes.

Book IV makes the categorical architecture explicit: `Reg` is a category, `POT` is a category/Grothendieck fibration, POT morphisms compose, and the persistence diagram is already a functor `D_pers : Reg -> POT`.

Most importantly for this gate, Book IV declares each representation layer `R_R` **initially empty** and says it may be populated only when a bridge theorem licenses it. This is positive architectural evidence that representation is contemplated, and simultaneously a hard anti-smuggling barrier against treating an arbitrary linearization as already canonical.

## 2. Book-V result is stronger than the prior design-space sketch

Book V explicitly defines a licensed target category `C` as a mathematical category to which a faithful realization functor from `POT` may be directed, provided an explicit bridge theorem licenses the functor. It names finite algebraic sector categories and **operator/semigroup categories** as examples.

It then defines a realization functor

`F : POT -> C`

which preserves POT-fibration structure, maps POT morphisms to morphisms of `C`, and must be licensed by an explicit bridge theorem.

Therefore a process-to-operator functor is not foreign to NFC architecture. The constitutional slot for it already exists.

However, Book V does not supply the SPEC-specific bridge theorem, target operator category, or probe-to-operator assignment. The definition of a lawful realization functor is a governance schema, not the missing theorem itself.

Call this category `Path_SPEC`.

This is a theorem-form reconstruction of already certified structure, not a new physical postulate.

For example, after choosing a commutative semiring/ring/field `k`, form the free `k`-linearization `k[Path_SPEC]`: hom-sets are freely generated by the certified arrows/paths and composition is bilinearly extended from lawful path composition.

This construction has the expected universal property: any functor from `Path_SPEC` into a `k`-linear category factors uniquely through the free `k`-linearization.

The source-forced support and episode provenance are preserved. The ledger can remain as an additive grading/label without being converted into coefficients.

Thus a genuine representation theorem exists **relative to the declared algebraic target choice**.
