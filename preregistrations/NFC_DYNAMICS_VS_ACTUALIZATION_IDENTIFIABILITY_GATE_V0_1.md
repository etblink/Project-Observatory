## Question

After ordinary branch dynamics or a separately certified dynamical law fixes transition-channel propensities on a prospectively fixed quotient-visible outcome partition, does NFC require or support a separately identifiable physical actualization law that changes observable predictions?

## Null

`H0 = NO_SEPARATELY_IDENTIFIABLE_ACTUALIZATION_LAYER`

A separately named actualization layer is not physically identifiable if it merely renames, normalizes, samples from, or restates the transition probabilities already supplied by the dynamics and makes no additional observable prediction.

## Competing possibilities

- `A__SEPARATE_ACTUALIZATION_LAYER_EMPIRICALLY_IDENTIFIABLE`: there exists an additional actualization law whose contribution survives conditioning on the complete declared dynamical state and changes at least one observable/interventional distribution.
- `B__SEPARATE_ACTUALIZATION_LAYER_FORMALLY_DISTINCT_BUT_EMPIRICALLY_NONIDENTIFIABLE`: a distinct formal object can be written, but all observable distributions remain identical to those generated by the dynamics alone.
- `C__NO_SEPARATELY_IDENTIFIABLE_ACTUALIZATION_LAYER`: once the complete declared dynamics is fixed, the actualization layer contributes no independent observable law at audited scope.
- `D__CURRENT_DYNAMICS_INCOMPLETE_SO_IDENTIFIABILITY_NOT_ADJUDICABLE`: the branch dynamics are insufficiently specified to separate dynamical incompleteness from actualization.
- `E__SCOPE_OR_BRANCH_SPECIFIC_EXTRA_LAYER_ONLY`: an additional law is identifiable only in a declared branch/scope, not universally.

## Identifiability criterion

Let `D` denote the complete declared dynamical specification, `A` a proposed actualization law, `I` the intervention/preparation protocol, and `O` the observable outcome map.

A is separately empirically identifiable only if there exists at least one prospectively specified regime such that

`P(O | D, A, I) != P(O | D, I)`.

Equivalently, if for every admissible intervention and observable map

`P(O | D, A, I) = P(O | D, I)`,

then A is empirically redundant at that scope.

## Anti-circularity rules

1. Do not define the dynamics as incomplete merely because an outcome is stochastic.
2. Do not define actualization as the act of sampling from a probability law and then count the existence of a sampled outcome as a new prediction.
3. Do not infer physical distinctness from metaphysical or interpretive distinctness alone.
4. A hidden variable or selector counts as a new physical layer only if it changes an observable/interventional distribution or yields a separately testable structural consequence.
5. A reparameterization of the same probability kernel does not count as a new law.
6. Post-hoc partition changes are forbidden.

## Required guardrail

It does not prove that metaphysical questions about why one outcome occurs are meaningless, nor that all possible future completion theories are equivalent.
