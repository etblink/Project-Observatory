## Mandatory adversarial tests
- Additivity: witness weights must reconstruct I_n exactly, not merely bound it.
- Quotient visibility: no raw token identity or hidden labels.
- Stock/increment firewall: I_n witness stock cannot be identified with B_n step increment.
- Transfer provenance: witness persistence must descend through declared transfer maps.
- Split/merge handling: provenance may branch or merge only with explicit lawful semantics.
- Loss semantics: disappearance from a collar is not automatically annihilation unless certified.
- No contraction smuggling: no retention factor <1 may be assumed.
- No endpoint fitting: no witness definition may depend on whether NS later blows up or remains regular.

## Stop rule
If exact additive witness decomposition is not derivable, identify the smallest missing bridge and stop before inventing weights or probabilistic semantics.
