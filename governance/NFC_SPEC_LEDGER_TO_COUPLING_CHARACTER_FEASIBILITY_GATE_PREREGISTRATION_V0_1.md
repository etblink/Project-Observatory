## Question

Does the accepted episode-level SPEC ledger-enriched relational action admit a source-privileged scalar character / representation that can convert additive transition bookkeeping into probe-dependent numerical coupling coefficients without using outcome frequencies or importing an external amplitude law?

## Accepted input

The accepted hierarchy is:

`P -> R_P -> J_P^B -> J_P^ledger -> ? scalar character -> ? physical interaction operator -> ? transition strengths -> transition kernel`.

Frozen SPEC already provides episode-level transition ledgers and additive composition along lawful probe chains. No scalar physical coefficient has yet been derived.

## Candidate scalarization classes

1. `ADDITIVE_LINEAR`: real linear functional on ledger components.
2. `MULTIPLICATIVE_CHARACTER`: additive-ledger to multiplicative-positive weight, e.g. exponential character.
3. `BOOK_IV_NORMALIZED`: scalarization using the canonical transport invariant `I_nl` and its normalization coefficients `(lambda,mu,nu)=(1,1,beta)` with `beta=log_2(3/2)`.
4. `YM_HEAT_SEMIGROUP`: use of the YM heat-semigroup precedent `exp(-beta Delta_A)` as a possible transform pattern.
5. `RH_HALF_DENSITY`: RH-style branch-local transformed-probe weighting / half-density architecture as a pattern only.
6. `YM_BILINEAR`: scalarization via transport-compatible invariant bilinear form after lawful landing in its domain.
7. `LING_COMPOSITION`: check whether grammar/compositional congruence constrains scalar characters by composition alone.
8. `SYMMETRY_INVARIANCE`: source/branch symmetries as possible uniqueness constraints on characters.

## Mandatory success conditions for a source-privileged coupling character

A qualifying character `chi` must:

1. be defined on frozen, quotient-visible ledger/action data;
2. be invariant under licensed presentation changes;
3. respect lawful probe-chain composition in the declared sense;
4. have its scalar codomain fixed by source or licensed branch structure;
5. have all normalization constants source-fixed rather than fit;
6. be probe/channel sensitive enough to distinguish unequal transition strengths where the frozen structure distinguishes channels;
7. land in, or admit a separately proved bridge into, the SPEC/YM/SM operator arena;
8. carry a theorem-level reason why its scalar output is a coupling/amplitude/strength rather than merely a bookkeeping score;
9. use no empirical branching ratios or post hoc calibration;
10. preserve all prior adverse results, including rejection of raw equal-child weighting.

## Mandatory adversarial tests

- `BETA_REPURPOSING`: Book-IV `beta` is a canonical transport/ledger normalization; do not assume it is a transition-amplitude constant.
- `HEAT_KERNEL_REPURPOSING`: a YM heat semigroup does not automatically imply a SPEC transition kernel.
- `EXPONENTIAL_CONVENIENCE`: additivity mathematically admits exponentials, but physical selection of one exponential character requires a theorem.
- `LINEAR_FUNCTIONAL_FREEDOM`: multiple linear functionals may exist on multi-component ledgers.
- `RH_ANALOGY`: RH half-density/arithmetic weights stay branch-local absent transfer theorem.
- `BILINEAR_DOMAIN`: YM invariant bilinear form acts only after a probe/channel is lawfully represented in its domain.
- `SYMMETRY_DEGENERACY`: invariance may force equality only within symmetry orbits, not across inequivalent channels.
- `COMPOSITION_ONLY`: multiplicativity under composition can leave a continuous family of characters.
- `DIMENSIONAL_MEANING`: dimensionless bookkeeping and physical coupling cannot be identified merely by notation.
- `EMPIRICAL_RETROFIT`: no observed branching fractions may select among candidate characters.

## Strongest positive target

Derive a unique or finite, source-selected family of scalar characters `chi` such that lawful ledger concatenation corresponds to the required scalar composition and such that `chi` is already licensed as physically meaningful in SPEC/YM/SM.

## Strongest negative target

Show that frozen constraints permit a nontrivial family of inequivalent characters (for example a continuous exponential family after any admissible scalar ledger projection), with no theorem selecting one member or granting it coupling authority.
