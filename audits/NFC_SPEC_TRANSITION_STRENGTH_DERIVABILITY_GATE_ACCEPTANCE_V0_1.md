## Minimality review

PASS.

The execution correctly distinguishes three already-frozen SPEC capabilities:

1. identifying quotient-visible response classes;
2. identifying transition-accessible spectral packets through generator compatibility;
3. recording certified before/after response changes in a transition ledger.

None of those statements supplies a child-specific prospective strength function. The frozen text further blocks an illicit shortcut by explicitly stating that transition-ledger additivity is bookkeeping rather than physical amplitude/intensity additivity.

The proposed missing object `M_SPEC(parent, probe, child_i) -> a_i` is therefore a legitimate minimal placeholder for the absent channel-coupling content. It does not prejudge the eventual mathematical representation.

## Existing operator/eigenvector reversal

PASS.

The audit considered whether frozen operator structure might already contain enough information implicitly. SPEC identifies spectral modes/eigenclasses and the probe-visible subset of those modes, but no frozen theorem maps an eigenvector overlap, projector coefficient, response signature, spectral gap, or transport invariant into physical relative transition strength.

The absence is theorem-level, not merely terminological. A future proof could potentially construct such a map from already-frozen objects, but that proof is not present in the frozen corpus.

Accordingly the result should not be strengthened to claim a genuinely new primitive is logically necessary.

## YM/SM import review

PASS.

YM contributes operator/gauge/spectral-gap/coercive structure and SM contributes gauge/harmonization/superselection structure. These can constrain allowed sectors and modes. No frozen theorem in the audited stack exports quantitative transition amplitudes or branching weights into SPEC.

The transport normalization constants in YM/Book IV govern declared transport/coercive structure and are not probability-normalization constants for competing spectroscopy channels.

## Constructive nonforcing review

PASS.

Holding fixed the parent/probe/response partition, accessible spectral packets, spectra, gaps, ledgers, and branch-governance structure while varying positive channel weights leaves the cited frozen theorem content unchanged. Therefore the weights are not forced by those theorems.

This is enough to support Outcome D at audited scope.

## External-physics guardrail

PASS.

At the same time, it does not claim such external physics is permanently required. An expanded NFC bridge may still be possible.
