## Question
Can the frozen NFC spine plus the frozen NS branch's existing Book V/VI realization and continuum-interface machinery conditionally supply a lawful physical-source localization map from a prospectively declared forcing decomposition into NFC-NS windows, without assuming the source-to-window correspondence being proved?

## Candidate object
Let `A_phys` index a prospectively declared physical forcing decomposition and let `{W_k}` be the NFC-NS window family. A qualifying localization bridge is either

`R_F \subseteq A_phys \times {W_k}`

or equivalently

`Lambda_F : A_phys -> P({W_k})`,

with the interpretation that `alpha R_F W_k` iff the physical source component indexed by `alpha` is lawfully represented in NS window `W_k`.

## Mandatory requirements
A qualifying theorem/schema must satisfy all of:

1. **Prospective source declaration** — `A_phys`, its decomposition rule, and source provenance are declared before outcome inspection.
2. **Typed localization** — physical source components remain external-source objects and are not silently identified with endogenous defect events.
3. **Book V legitimacy** — the map is realized through an explicit branch/realization witness rather than a target-side assertion.
4. **Book VI interface discipline** — continuum support/scale information is transferred only through an explicit licensed interface.
5. **Support fidelity** — if a source component is outside the represented support of a window, it may not be assigned to that window merely for algebraic convenience.
6. **Refinement compatibility** — lawful refinement/coarsening of physical decomposition or NFC windows must have a declared compatibility law.
7. **Representation invariance** — presentation changes that preserve the physical source component and the certified window cannot alter incidence arbitrarily.
8. **No empirical retrofit** — no localization choices may depend on known blowup/safe outcomes.
9. **OpenAI negative-control compatibility** — the OpenAI forced-blowup construction is used only as a frozen stress test, never to choose the map post hoc.
10. **No premature transport weighting** — localization alone does not inherit endogenous transport weights unless a separate source-transport theorem is proved.

## Mandatory adversarial tests
- **Identity fallacy:** a physical-space support set is not automatically an NFC window.
- **Scale fallacy:** matching length/time scales does not itself prove lawful representation.
- **Partition fallacy:** a partition of unity does not by itself identify source semantics with window semantics.
- **Defect conflation:** source entries may not become endogenous defect entries by relabeling.
- **Continuum inversion:** one may not infer source-side primitives from target continuum coordinates without a licensed inverse bridge.
- **Outcome leakage:** no use of OpenAI's known blowup to select localization.
- **Refinement instability:** arbitrary changes under equivalent decompositions count against canonicity.
