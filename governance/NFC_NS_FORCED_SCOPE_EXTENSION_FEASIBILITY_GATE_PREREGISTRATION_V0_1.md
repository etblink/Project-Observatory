## Question

Can the frozen NFC NS architecture be extended **in schema** to a smoothly forced Navier–Stokes scope without circularity or hidden supplementation, by introducing an explicit forcing/source contribution at every load-bearing layer while preserving the existing separation between source-descended structure, obstruction control, continuation, and endpoint promotion?

This operation does not prove a forced regularity theorem. It asks only whether a coherent extension schema exists and which new scientific objects remain missing.

## Mandatory layers

A viable schema must address all five layers separately:

1. discrete balance identity;
2. obstruction/defect ledger;
3. common-state bridge;
4. continuation criterion;
5. master inequality / quantitative sufficient condition.

## Required source object

The forced extension must introduce a new explicit source descriptor `F` (or equivalent) that is:

- prospectively declared;
- quotient-visible or carried through a separately licensed continuum/source bridge;
- distinct from the endogenous NFC defect burden;
- not inferred from the later blowup/regularity outcome;
- equipped with a declared support/time profile and regularity class at the scope where the forced theorem is stated.

## OpenAI negative-control benchmark

The FCP-admitted OpenAI construction is frozen prospectively as a negative control with:

- `u_0 = 0`;
- `nu > 0` arbitrary;
- smooth compactly supported forcing;
- bounded kinetic energy / bounded `L^2`;
- finite-time `L^infinity` blowup.

A candidate forced-scope NFC schema fails if it would certify regularity for that construction while all of its stated hypotheses remain satisfied.

## Mandatory adversarial tests

- **Source-separation test:** forcing may not be hidden inside the existing defect term by relabeling.
- **Zero-initial-data test:** the criterion may not become automatically sufficient merely because `u_0=0`.
- **Energy-insufficiency test:** bounded `L^2` alone may not imply endpoint regularity.
- **Common-state augmentation test:** same-state comparison must specify whether the source state/profile is part of the compared state.
- **UWB independence test:** compact forcing support may not be equated with UWB.
- **Duhamel/work-term test:** the forced balance must contain an explicit source/work contribution or equivalent theorem-visible object.
- **No-retrofit test:** source norm/control functional may not be chosen after observing blowup solely to exclude the OpenAI benchmark.
- **Reduction test:** setting forcing to zero should recover the unforced architecture without altering its existing statuses.
