## Question

What is the minimum additional physical object that could lawfully map a declared SPEC probe into the already-constrained SPEC/YM/SM operator arena, while preserving quotient visibility, composition/accounting, and falsifiability, and without encoding transition amplitudes, branching ratios, or observed frequencies by construction?

## Scope

This is a candidate-feasibility gate, not canon admission and not a truth claim. It may construct candidate schemas and reject them. It may not mutate frozen NFC canon or treat SM/YM structural coupling formulas as empirical validation of a probe-current law.

## Required baseline

Accepted upstream hierarchy:

`probe P -> certified episode -> Path_SPEC -> free representation relative to declared target -> constrained SPEC operator-target family -> ? physical interaction object -> ? coupling/strength law -> transition kernel`

The current gate addresses only the `? physical interaction object` slot.

## Candidate classes

1. `I1_OPERATOR_VALUED_CURRENT` — a quotient-visible map `J : P -> Op(A_SPEC)` into the constrained operator arena.
2. `I2_BOUNDED_PERTURBATION` — probe represented by a bounded perturbation `V_P` of `L_SPEC` or of an admissible operator packet.
3. `I3_GENERATOR_DERIVATION` — probe represented by a derivation/commutator action on the operator algebra.
4. `I4_SOURCE_TERM` — probe represented as a source/current term entering a declared branch equation or generator.
5. `I5_YM_SM_CURRENT` — probe landing through already-declared YM/SM gauge/matter current structure, only where such structure is frozen and scoped.
6. `I6_RH_STYLE_TRANSFORMED_PROBE` — transformed-probe/operator-origin architecture with no imported arithmetic weights.
7. `I7_LING_STYLE_CONTEXT_ACTION` — context/action object as precursor but not automatically physical.
8. `I8_DIRECT_WEIGHTED_EDGE_OPERATOR` — directly assign numerical coefficients to probe transitions; presumptively suspect for hidden amplitudes.
9. `I9_NO_MINIMAL_PHYSICAL_CANDIDATE`.

## Candidate requirements

A viable minimal candidate must satisfy:

- `C1_DECLARED_DOMAIN`: acts on the already-constrained SPEC/YM/SM arena.
- `C2_QUOTIENT_VISIBILITY`: source-equivalent probes/states give equivalent interaction objects.
- `C3_COMPOSITION`: lawful probe composition induces a controlled composition/update law.
- `C4_NO_AMPLITUDE_SMUGGLING`: no transition strength or observed frequency is encoded in the definition.
- `C5_NO_PROBABILITY_SMUGGLING`: no stochastic kernel is built in.
- `C6_OPERATOR_COMPATIBILITY`: compatible with `L_SPEC`, its domain/invariants, and the accepted target family.
- `C7_PHYSICAL_INTERPRETABILITY`: unlike a purely formal free representation, the object is explicitly proposed to represent an intervention/interaction.
- `C8_FALSIFICATION_EXPOSURE`: later coupling laws built from it could in principle disagree with observed responses.
- `C9_SCOPE_VISIBILITY`: any YM/SM imports retain their exact conditional scope.
- `C10_PARAMETER_DISCIPLINE`: free parameters, if any, are explicit and not fitted silently to the same branching data later used to test the model.
