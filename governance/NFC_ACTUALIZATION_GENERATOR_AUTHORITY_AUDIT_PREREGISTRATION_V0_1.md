# NFC Actualization Generator Authority Audit — Preregistration v0.1

STATUS = PREREGISTERED__AUDIT_NOT_EXECUTED

## 1. Purpose

Test whether any quantity, invariant, ledger, ordering, extremum, or branch-local transition object already present in frozen NFC possesses source-supported authority to govern physical actualization among lawful histories.

This is not a search for quantities that can be mathematically normalized into a selector. The burden is stronger: the frozen source must license the candidate as physically actualization-relevant rather than merely admissibility-, persistence-, stability-, transport-, distinction-, classification-, or bookkeeping-relevant.

The common formal actualization interface may be represented as a map assigning, for each lawful post-observation state Z, a normalized measure A_Z over lawful continuation histories H(Z). Deterministic actualization is the Dirac special case; genuine stochastic actualization has nontrivial support. This formal interface does not itself supply physical authority.

## 3. Primary question

Does frozen NFC itself identify any already-defined internal quantity or structure as having the authority to determine or constrain the physical actualization map A_Z beyond ordinary admissibility/support restriction?

## 5. Authority criterion

A candidate Q counts as actualization-authoritative only if frozen source support establishes more than one or more of the following non-sufficient properties:
- Q is canonical;
- Q is monotone;
- Q is additive;
- Q is bounded;
- Q is extremized;
- Q is unique;
- Q determines an observational class;
- Q ranks admissible histories;
- Q appears in a transition equation;
- Q is physically suggestive;
- Q can be normalized into weights.

For actualization authority, source support must instead establish at least one of:

A. deterministic force: the physically actual continuation is required to be an argmin/argmax/fixed point/unique survivor selected by Q;
B. stochastic force: Q source-forces a normalized, physically interpreted nontrivial weighting over lawful continuations;
C. support force stronger than admissibility: Q source-forces elimination of otherwise lawful continuations specifically because they are not physically actualizable;
D. equivalent theorem-level force connecting Q to actual physical realization rather than structural classification.

The source must also make the scope and provenance of the authority explicit enough that it is not supplied by an undeclared bridge, external interpretation, or circular assumption of actualization.

## 6. Mandatory adversarial tests

### 6.1 Normalization fallacy test
If a nonnegative Q can be transformed into `w(h) ∝ exp(-Q(h))`, `1/(1+Q(h))`, or any similar normalized weight, that construction does not count unless NFC itself licenses that transformation and its physical interpretation.

### 6.2 Extremum fallacy test
If NFC proves that Q is minimized/maximized or that a minimal/maximal carrier exists, determine whether the extremum is a representation/carrier/stability fact or an actual-history law.

### 6.3 Ordering-vs-selection test
A preorder or ranking over histories does not select one actual history unless the source says the physically actual history is determined by that ordering.

### 6.4 Branch-to-source lifting test
Branch-local selection/dynamics do not acquire universal source authority without an explicit source-forcing lift.

### 6.5 Observation-backaction test
Probe-induced or observation-linked state change must be distinguished from a law choosing which post-probe continuation is physically actual.

### 6.6 Conservative completion test
If the frozen source remains compatible with at least two distinct actualization maps A_Z while all candidate-Q theorems remain true, Q does not possess source-forced actualization authority at the audited scope.

## 7. Outcome taxonomy

A = `SOURCE_FORCED_ACTUALIZATION_GENERATOR_IDENTIFIED`

B = `CONDITIONAL_OR_BRANCH_LOCAL_ACTUALIZATION_GENERATOR_IDENTIFIED`

C = `INTERNAL_QUANTITIES_CONSTRAIN_WITHOUT_ACTUALIZATION_AUTHORITY`

D = `PARTIAL_ACTUALIZATION_PRESSURE_IDENTIFIED__COMPLETION_LAW_STILL_REQUIRED`

E = `UNDERDETERMINED`

F = `REPAIR_REQUIRED`

Outcome C requires positive evidence that the strongest candidate quantities retain their existing theorem roles under multiple incompatible actualization completions, or explicit source language delimiting them to non-actualizing roles. Search failure alone is insufficient.

Outcome D requires a source-supported restriction on actualization stronger than ordinary admissibility but insufficient to determine A_Z.

## 8. Mandatory reversals

Pro-authority reversal: construct the strongest possible chain from an existing NFC quantity to physical actualization and identify the exact theorem where authority would enter.

Anti-authority reversal: demonstrate, where possible, two distinct actualization completions preserving the same frozen candidate quantities and theorem truths.

No NFC, FCP, or PGH scientific source is to be mutated during this audit.
