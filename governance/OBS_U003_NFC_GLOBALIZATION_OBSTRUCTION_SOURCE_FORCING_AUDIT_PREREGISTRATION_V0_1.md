## 1. Audit question

The controlling question is:

> **Does the frozen canonical NFC source, using only premises with valid force at the frozen boundary and without importing the historical universal-wavefunction claim or external state ontology, force any nontrivial structural obstruction to globally assembling its own source-licensed locally/stably admissible observational structures into a single source-licensed global object over a nontrivial declared scope? If so, what exact weaker obstruction is forced? If not, can source nonforcing be established by a lawful globalizing model or independence argument rather than inferred from silence?**

The audit is about source forcing. It is not an audit of ordinary quantum mechanics and does not presuppose that the relevant global object is a wavefunction.

## 2. Frozen neutral vocabulary

### 2.1 Source-licensed local diagram

A `LOCAL_DIAGRAM` is any diagram explicitly licensed by the frozen source consisting of local/regional/regime-level stabilized observational objects together with the restriction, inclusion, overlap, transfer, projection, or comparison maps that the source itself declares relevant.

No sheaf, Hilbert space, probability space, manifold, or vector bundle may be assumed merely because such language would make globalization familiar. Such structures count only if the frozen source licenses them at the exact audited scope.

### 2.2 Compatible local family

A `COMPATIBLE_LOCAL_FAMILY` is a family `x = {x_i}` of source-licensed local data satisfying every compatibility condition the frozen source actually requires on overlaps, inclusions, transfers, or projections.

Compatibility may not be strengthened during execution to make a positive theorem easier, nor weakened to manufacture a countermodel.

### 2.3 Global object

A `GLOBAL_OBJECT` for a compatible family is a single object `G` in a source-licensed ambient class/category together with source-licensed maps `r_i` such that:

```text
r_i(G) = x_i
```

for every index in the declared domain, with all required diagrammatic compatibility conditions commuting.

The notation is schematic. Execution must translate it into the source's own objects and morphisms before claiming a theorem.

A global object need not be unique unless the source explicitly requires uniqueness.

### 2.4 Canonical globalizer

A `CANONICAL_GLOBALIZER` is an assignment

```text
Gamma : COMPATIBLE_LOCAL_FAMILY -> GLOBAL_OBJECT
```

on a declared nontrivial domain such that the assignment is invariant under the source's own equivalence relation and natural/compatible under the admissible maps the source requires.

Canonicality is stronger than mere existence. The audit must never infer nonexistence from failure of canonical selection.

### 2.5 Obstruction classes

Every candidate obstruction discovered during execution must be classified before it can affect the controlling outcome:

- `O_EXISTENCE`: the source forces that at least one compatible local family in the claimed domain has **no** global object.
- `O_CANONICALITY`: global objects may exist, but the source forces that no canonical globalizer can exist on the claimed nontrivial domain.
- `O_REPRESENTATION`: globalization is impossible inside a **specific source-licensed representation category**, without claiming impossibility in every ambient class.
- `O_NONUNIQUENESS`: a compatible family does not determine a unique global object up to the source equivalence.
- `O_CERTIFICATION`: the source does not license promotion from local/scoped information to a global claim without a separate transfer/certificate.

`O_NONUNIQUENESS` alone is **not** equivalent to `O_EXISTENCE` or `O_CANONICALITY` unless the source explicitly makes uniqueness part of the required globalization notion.

`O_CERTIFICATION` is an epistemic/governance barrier, not automatically a structural no-go theorem. It may support Outcome B but cannot by itself establish Outcome A.

## 3. What counts as a nontrivial weaker globalization obstruction

Outcome A requires a theorem stronger than mere absence of a proof and weaker/more precise than the withdrawn historical slogan.

A result qualifies only if all of the following hold:

1. it proves `O_EXISTENCE`, `O_CANONICALITY`, or `O_REPRESENTATION` on a nonempty source-licensed domain;
2. the object class, local data, compatibility relation, and global target are explicitly source-licensed;
3. its premises do not contain the obstruction itself under a renamed condition;
4. every dependency has valid declared force at the claimed source scope;
5. no branch-specific result is silently promoted to source-wide force;
6. no external QM ontology or post-freeze diagnostic premise is imported;
7. if LS-2 or finite-interface sufficiency is used, it is used only at an independently certified scope consistent with accepted Observatory dependency `OBS-DP014`, never as an assumed universal source consequence;
8. the result genuinely forbids or blocks globalization rather than merely saying that the current text has not supplied a transfer theorem.

A theorem may qualify for Outcome B when it is valid only conditionally, only in a branch, only in a narrower representation class, or only as a certification/promotion barrier.

## 4. Historical-slogan firewall

The phrases `No Universal Wavefunction`, `universal wavefunction`, or equivalent historical labels have no special evidentiary force.

Execution must decompose any matching passage into the neutral obstruction classes above and audit the actual theorem statement and dependencies.

The following moves are forbidden:

- interpreting the historical title as stronger than the proof statement;
- treating later withdrawal as proof of source nonforcing;
- assuming that `wavefunction` means an ordinary Hilbert-space vector unless the source explicitly licenses that structure;
- converting local quotient noninvertibility into global nonexistence without an explicit implication;
- converting lack of a canonical inverse into lack of any global object;
- converting no-smuggling or status/promotion governance into an ontological impossibility theorem.

## 5. Frozen evidence and search procedure

### 5.1 Primary pass

Execution must inspect all seven spine books in order I-VII and construct a candidate globalization ledger.

The search is not keyword-exclusive, but the following neutral term families must be used as discovery aids:

```text
global, globally, universal, state, object, section,
glue, gluing, patch, patching, overlap, compatible, compatibility,
projection, inverse, singular, quotient, descent, source, fiber, fibration,
restriction, transfer, factor, factorization, unique, uniqueness,
obstruction, no-go, promotion, bridge, representation
```

Any candidate passage must be expanded to include:

- theorem/definition/standing-rule statement;
- declared status tag;
- complete listed hypotheses;
- proof or derivation neighborhood;
- every direct dependency needed for force;
- scope statement and failure frontier when present.

### 5.2 Scoped subsidiary pass

All ten branch books must be checked for:

- explicitly scoped globalization/no-globalization results;
- branch instances that might supply Outcome B;
- lawful globalized realizations that could inform an adversarial model;
- dependencies imported from the spine.

Branch text is not source-wide by default.

### 5.3 Authority pass

Authority/provenance files may resolve lifecycle/status competence but may not manufacture a theorem premise.

### 5.4 No cherry-picking rule

Execution may not stop after finding the first passage supporting an obstruction. It must search for source passages that cut the other way, including universal/source objects, lawful descent, reconstruction, projections, or realizations that could supply a globalization route or counterexample.

## 6. Premise-admissibility rules

Every premise in a claimed positive derivation must be entered in a premise ledger with:

```text

DEPENDENCIES
TARGET_CONTAINING = YES/NO
BRANCH_SPECIFIC = YES/NO
OBSERVATORY_FIREWALL_RELEVANCE
```

A premise is inadmissible for source-wide Outcome A if it is:

- `[C]` without its conditions independently established at the target scope;
- branch-specific without a valid transfer theorem;
- historical/remark-only/speculative;
- later noncanonical material;
- equivalent to the desired globalization obstruction itself;
- dependent on universal LS-2 source forcing contrary to the accepted U002 constraint;
- dependent on external state/quantum ontology not licensed by source.

## 7. Positive-forcing burden

The positive pass occurs before the negative pass.

Outcome A may be assigned only if execution constructs a complete noncircular source trace:

```text
FROZEN_SOURCE_PREMISES
        |
        v
SOURCE-LICENSED_LOCAL_DIAGRAM
        |
        v
COMPATIBLE_LOCAL_FAMILY DOMAIN
        |
        v
PROVED O_EXISTENCE / O_CANONICALITY / O_REPRESENTATION
```

The proof must identify exactly which obstruction class is forced and on what scope.

A statement that globalization has not been certified, that a transfer theorem is required, or that inverse selection is noncanonical is not sufficient unless the source also proves the exact obstruction implication required by Section 3.

## 8. Adversarial nonforcing burden

Outcome C may **not** be assigned because:

- the historical theorem was later withdrawn;
- no proof was found;
- the relevant words do not appear;
- a candidate positive proof has a defect.

Outcome C requires at least one of:

1. an explicit lawful model satisfying the relevant frozen antecedent source premises in which a nontrivial compatible local-data domain admits global objects in a way that defeats every candidate source-wide obstruction discovered in the positive pass;
2. a model with a total canonical globalizer on the audited domain, when the strongest claimed obstruction is canonicality;
3. a parameterized family establishing that the candidate obstruction is not a logical consequence of the frozen antecedents;
4. an equivalently strong formal independence/non-implication argument.

The countermodel must preserve, where relevant to the positive claim:

- source equivalence/no-smuggling discipline;
- admitted quotient/refinement structure;
- declared local compatibility conditions;
- any finite/stabilized structure actually used as an antecedent;
- source-licensed morphism/transfer rules used by the positive route.

It may not evade the theorem by simply violating one of its genuine antecedents.

## 9. Relationship to OBS-U001 and OBS-U002

Accepted Observatory findings are **audit firewalls**, not source premises.

`OBS-U001` forbids the Observatory from silently inventing an actual-history selector while analyzing globalization. It does not prove any globalization result.

`OBS-U002`/`OBS-DP014` forbids the Observatory from treating LS-2-equivalent finite-interface sufficiency as universally source-forced. A source passage may still use LS-2 lawfully at an independently certified conditional scope.

Neither prior Observatory result may be cited as though it were an NFC theorem.

## 10. Frozen outcome taxonomy

Exactly one controlling outcome must be assigned at execution close:

```text
A__WEAKER_GLOBALIZATION_OBSTRUCTION_SOURCE_FORCED
B__ONLY_CONDITIONAL_OR_SCOPED_GLOBALIZATION_OBSTRUCTION_ESTABLISHED
C__SOURCE_NONFORCING_ESTABLISHED_AT_AUDITED_SCOPE
D__CURRENT_SOURCE_INSUFFICIENT_TO_DECIDE
E__SOURCE_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT_PREVENTS_ADJUDICATION
```

### Outcome A

Use only when a nontrivial source-wide obstruction satisfying Section 3 is proved from admissible frozen premises.

### Outcome B

Use when valid obstruction-like content exists, but only under explicit extra hypotheses, branch/scoped domains, representation-specific scope, or certification/promotion limits insufficient for source-wide A.

### Outcome C

Use only with the explicit countermodel/independence burden of Section 8 and only after the strongest positive route has been tested.

### Outcome D

Use when neither a valid source-wide proof nor a valid nonforcing construction/independence result can be established from the frozen source.

### Outcome E

Use when source identity, theorem scope, same-tier contradiction, or target definition is defective enough that A-D cannot be soundly adjudicated without repair or corpus expansion.

## 11. Outcome precedence

Apply the following precedence rules:

1. A valid source-wide proof defeats C.
2. A valid lawful countermodel to the exact claimed universal/source implication defeats A.
3. B does not control when a valid source-wide A proof exists.
4. C controls over B only when the audit target is universal/source forcing and the countermodel defeats that universal implication while preserving any legitimate scoped B results.
5. E controls only when the defect blocks adjudication itself; a localized defective proof route does not force E if another independent argument adjudicates the target.
6. D is not a default negative result; it is used only after both positive and adversarial burdens have been attempted and remain unresolved.

## 12. Execution order

A future separately authorized execution must proceed in this order:

1. integrity verification against the frozen manifest;
2. full source map and candidate obstruction ledger;
3. premise ledger and obstruction-class classification;
4. strongest positive source-forcing derivation attempt;
5. adversarial globalizing-model / independence attempt;
6. scope, equivalence, branch-promotion, and prior-Observatory-firewall audit;
7. controlling outcome adjudication;
8. execution record and hard stop.

No repair search, new axiom proposal, external literature search, or NFC mutation is part of execution.

## 13. Stopping rule

Once one controlling A-E outcome is established to the preregistered burden, execution stops.

The following are separate future operations and are not implicitly authorized:

- repairing a defective NFC theorem;
- changing the status of the historical `No Universal Wavefunction` claim;
- adding a new globalization axiom;
- importing a sheaf/Hilbert/probability formalism;
- changing branch or spine theorem labels;
- FCP intake/recomputation;
- PGH empirical work;
- HiVenues work;
- selecting OBS-U009 or any later Observatory target.
