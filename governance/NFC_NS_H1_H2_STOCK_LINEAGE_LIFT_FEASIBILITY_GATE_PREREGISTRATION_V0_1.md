## Question
Does the type/definition of an active interface-stock witness, together with frozen H1 quotient loss and H2 finite-collar mixing, force a quantitative lineage-population lift from H1/H2 loss mechanisms to active-stock resolution rates?

## Candidate lift strengths
L1 FULL H1 LIFT: every active stock witness is a non-invariant boundary/interface mismatch to which the H1 quantitative rate applies.
L2 PARTIAL H1 LIFT: only an explicitly certified subset of active stock witnesses lies in the H1 population; quantify the fraction if source-forced.
L3 FULL H2 LIFT: H2 collision/mixing acts on the full active stock lineage population at the certified c2 rate.
L4 CONDITIONAL H2 LIFT: H2 applies only to incompatible active-stock lineage pairs, with no guaranteed population fraction.
L5 EVENTWISE MECHANISM LIFT ONLY: H1/H2 events can be recognized when they occur on active lineages, but no positive fraction of active-stock mass is forced into those events.
L6 NO LIFT.

## Mandatory tests
1. Active interface burden is collar defect load, but defect-load status alone may not imply H1 non-invariance.
2. Persistent quotient-visible witnesses may remain invariant under lawful transfer; H1 cannot erase invariant content by definition.
3. H2 requires incompatible patterns; a stock population may be mutually compatible or concentrated in one stable type.
4. No lower stock-loss rate may be inferred from alphabet finiteness alone.
5. Quantitative H1/H2 constants certified against mathfrakD may not be retargeted to M/I without a population relation.
6. Identity-transfer/no-generation stock persistence remains an adversarial control.
7. Any finite-state or UWB assumption must retain its declared conditional status.
