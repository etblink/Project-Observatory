## 5. Deterministic-vs-stochastic adjudication

The execution's central subtlety is valid with one required wording guardrail.

Mathematically, a deterministic selector can be represented as a Dirac probability measure/kernel. Therefore K1 and K2 are not disjoint mathematical formalisms under an arbitrarily broad measure-theoretic umbrella.

However, the audit's claim is **physical-completion irreducibility**, not formal non-embeddability:

- deterministic actualization requires no nontrivial probability weights among multiple histories;
- genuinely stochastic actualization does require such nontrivial weights plus normalization/consistency and a physical interpretation of those weights;
- frozen NFC does not force the additional stochastic structure;
- frozen NFC also does not force determinism.

Thus no one of the two is justified as the physically weaker-and-general completion without adding a substantive commitment absent from the source.

Accepted statement:

> Given the accepted frozen-scope source nonforcing result, NFC admits at least two physically inequivalent classes of possible post-freeze actualization completion: deterministic history selection and genuinely stochastic history weighting/selection. A common measure-theoretic notation can embed the deterministic case as a Dirac measure, but this does not erase the extra physical commitment introduced by nontrivial stochastic weights. Frozen NFC privileges neither class. Variational, recursive, constraint-accumulation, initial-condition, branch-local, superselection, and meta-selector constructions qualify only insofar as they ultimately supply or reduce to a genuine history selector or normalized history measure without hidden burden relocation.
