## Controlling prior result

The immediately prior accepted audit established, at the audited frozen scope, that no actualization-relevant source certificate `S_A` is derivable from frozen NFC without adding scientifically substantive new physical content.

This operation does **not** reopen that finding. It asks what broad classes of new physical content could, in principle, close the actualization gap while preserving NFC's no-smuggling, quotient, provenance, transfer, and failure-exposure disciplines.

## Audit question

> What are the materially distinct classes of genuinely new physical content that could supply an NFC actualization source certificate or actualization law, and which classes are scientifically preferable under predeclared criteria **before** any particular law is invented or selected?

The audit is a design-space classification and feasibility gate. It is not authorization to write a new NFC law, mutate the frozen canon, fit data, or choose a favored ontology.

## Candidate class taxonomy

The following candidate classes are frozen before comparative evaluation.

### K1 — Deterministic law

A source- or branch-visible law that maps a lawful post-observation state and declared auxiliaries to one actual continuation history or one equivalence class of actual continuations.

### K2 — Genuinely stochastic law

A source- or branch-visible normalized physical probability measure/kernel over lawful continuation histories with nontrivial support in at least some regime.

### K3 — Variational/extremal law

A new physical principle assigning actualization authority to minimization/maximization/stationarity of a declared functional. This class counts as distinct only if the extremal principle has physical authority not already reducible to existing NFC admissibility/stability bookkeeping.

### K4 — Dynamical attractor/fixed-point law

A new physical law under which repeated lawful evolution drives admissible states/histories toward a physically privileged attractor, fixed point, absorbing class, or asymptotic basin. If unique, this may instantiate K1; if basin weights are physical, it may instantiate K2. The audit must record whether K4 is genuinely independent or merely an implementation form.

### K5 — Symmetry-breaking / boundary / initial-condition actualization law

A new physical principle under which globally or locally admissible alternatives are resolved by a law selecting boundary data, initial data, superselection sector, spontaneous-symmetry-breaking outcome, or equivalent condition. Merely stipulating an initial condition does not qualify; the physical rule selecting or weighting it must be supplied.

### K6 — Observation-coupled actualization law

A new physical rule linking a lawful NFC internal observation/probe transition and persistent record to actualization among post-interaction lawful continuations, without illicit inversion of observational equivalence.

### K7 — Empirically anchored phenomenological law

A deliberately non-fundamental law introduced at a declared physical regime because a stable empirical regularity demands it. It must be externally testable and must not be relabeled as source-forced.

### K8 — Meta-consistency / self-consistency / global constraint law

A new physical rule under which only globally self-consistent histories are actualizable, potentially through fixed-point, consistency, closure, or whole-history constraint. It must do more than existing admissibility and cannot merely rename the lawful-history set.

### K9 — Hybrid/composite law

A law combining two or more classes above. Hybridization is not automatically preferable; each extra mechanism incurs an additional assumption/provenance burden.

### K10 — No justified completion class yet

The scientifically correct result may be that no candidate class currently earns enough support to justify construction of a concrete actualizer.

## Evaluation criteria

Every class must be evaluated against the same criteria.

C1. **Gap closure** — can the class in principle map lawful multiplicity to physical actualization rather than only constrain/classify it?

C2. **NFC compatibility** — can it preserve observational quotient discipline, anti-inversion, persistence, branch legitimacy, and transfer governance?

C3. **Assumption cost** — how much genuinely new physical structure must be added beyond frozen NFC?

C4. **Noncircularity** — does the mechanism explain actualization without assuming the actual outcome, selector, or weights it is supposed to derive?

C5. **Scope clarity** — can source-level, branch-level, regime-level, and phenomenological force be kept distinct?

C6. **Ten-branch compatibility** — can the law coexist with all ten frozen branch programs without silently invalidating their certified content?

C7. **Deterministic/stochastic neutrality** — does the class illicitly choose one side of the previously accepted parity without new justification?

C8. **Empirical discriminability** — can concrete instantiations generate failure-exposed observable consequences under matched auxiliaries?

C9. **Parameter economy** — does the class avoid unconstrained free functions, hidden measures, arbitrary priors, or branch-by-branch tuning?

C10. **Compositional consistency** — can sequential observations/interactions yield coherent actualization without contradiction or normalization failure?

C11. **Representation invariance** — does the mechanism act on quotient-visible/branch-visible structure rather than presentation artifacts?

C12. **Failure exposure** — can the proposal state what result would refute, downgrade, or render the mechanism unnecessary?

No numeric weighted score is preregistered. The audit will use dominance and failure analysis, not a tunable composite score.

## Required adversarial tests

### Test A — Reduction test

Determine whether K3, K4, K5, K6, or K8 ultimately reduce to K1 or K2 once they become complete actualization laws. If so, preserve their mechanistic value but do not count notational repackaging as a new physical completion class.

### Test B — Hidden-choice test

For every class, ask whether unresolved choice has merely been moved into a functional, prior, initial condition, boundary condition, branch selection, basin choice, measure, or meta-selector.

### Test C — Conservative-compatibility test

Determine whether adding a representative law from the class can leave frozen NFC theorem truths intact. Compatibility does not confer truth or authority.

### Test D — Ten-branch stress test

Check all ten branches for direct contradiction, hidden scope contamination, or dependence on the absence of the proposed mechanism.

### Test E — Empirical ceiling test

Determine the minimum additional specification required before the class can make an observation-level prediction distinguishable from rival classes.

### Test F — No-preference reversal

Argue the strongest case that no class should presently be preferred and that K10 is the correct routing result.

### Test G — Minimal-extension reversal

Argue the strongest case for the smallest assumption-bearing class that can close actualization while remaining failure-exposed.

## Decision rule

A class may advance to concrete candidate generation only if it:

1. genuinely closes the actualization type gap in principle;
2. survives hidden-choice and noncircularity tests;
3. is compatible with frozen NFC at its declared scope;
4. has a credible path to empirical or theorem-level failure exposure; and
5. is not strictly dominated by another class on assumption cost, clarity, and discriminability.

Passing this gate does not make a class part of NFC and does not authorize canon mutation.
