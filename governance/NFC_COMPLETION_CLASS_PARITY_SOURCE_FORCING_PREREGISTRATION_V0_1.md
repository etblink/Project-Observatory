## 3. Frozen question

> Does frozen canonical NFC, from its own accepted premises and without importing a new actualization postulate, force a physically meaningful asymmetry between deterministic history selection and genuinely stochastic history weighting/selection?

The audit is not asking whether deterministic or stochastic mathematics appears in NFC. It is asking whether any source principle supplies **completion-class privilege** with physical force.

## 4. Completion classes under test

K1__DETERMINISTIC_HISTORY_SELECTOR

A law that, for the relevant admissible-history domain H, selects one history h* (or one physically equivalent history class) with source-forced authority.

K2__GENUINELY_STOCHASTIC_HISTORY_MEASURE_OR_KERNEL

A law that assigns nontrivial normalized weights/probabilities to multiple histories or transitions and gives those weights physical actualization significance.

A Dirac measure representation of K1 does not count as evidence for K2. K2 requires nontrivial stochastic weights plus physical interpretation.

## 5. What counts as parity breaking

A qualifying parity-breaking principle must satisfy all of the following:

P1__SOURCE_BOUND: derivable from frozen canonical NFC, not post-freeze diagnostics or external theory.

P2__CLASS_DISCRIMINATING: rules out, renders noncanonical, or makes physically redundant one of K1/K2 while preserving the other.

P3__PHYSICAL_FORCE: concerns actualization rather than mere notation, representation, calculational convenience, epistemic uncertainty, ensemble bookkeeping, or branch-local modeling choice.

P4__NONCIRCULAR: does not assume deterministic or stochastic actualization in the premise used to prefer it.

P5__NO_RELOCATION: does not move the choice into undeclared initial conditions, branch selection, a hidden random seed, a probability interpretation, an unexplained measure, or a meta-selector.

P6__SCOPE_MATCH: applies to the open universal/history-level completion problem rather than only to one branch-local model unless a source-forced lift is proved.

P7__REPRESENTATION_RESISTANT: survives equivalent reformulation; the preference is not an artifact of writing deterministic laws as delta measures or stochastic laws as deterministic dynamics on an enlarged hidden-variable state.

P8__COUNTERMODEL_EXPOSED: the claimed preference must survive an adversarial construction of an alternative completion consistent with the same frozen source commitments.

## 7. Required adversarial readings

### Pro-deterministic reading

Construct the strongest source-bound case that NFC's architecture privileges K1. Candidate arguments may include uniqueness/canonicality, fixed-point or extremal structure, PASS/no-backreaction, source universality, or branch-closure uniqueness.

The audit must distinguish uniqueness of representation/carrier/endpoint from uniqueness of actual history.

### Pro-stochastic reading

Construct the strongest source-bound case that NFC's architecture privileges K2. Candidate arguments may include finite observational quotients, branching/multiplicity, stochastic branch dynamics, ensemble structure, probability kernels, entropy/information language, or irreducible observational underdetermination.

The audit must distinguish stochastic model data or epistemic mixtures from a source-forced physical measure over actual histories.

### Parity-preserving reversal

Attempt to construct two post-freeze completion extensions—one K1 and one genuinely K2—that preserve all frozen canonical NFC commitments while differing only in the added actualization law. If both are conservative with respect to the frozen theorem corpus, that is strong evidence for parity preservation.

### Hidden-variable / enlarged-state reversal

Test whether apparent stochastic privilege disappears under deterministic enlargement, and whether apparent deterministic privilege disappears under stochastic coarse-graining. Neither representation trick may decide physical completion class without additional source authority.

## 8. Outcome taxonomy

Exactly one primary outcome must be selected.

A__DETERMINISTIC_COMPLETION_SOURCE_PRIVILEGED

Frozen NFC contains a qualifying principle satisfying P1–P8 that privileges K1 over genuine K2 at the relevant universal/history scope.

B__STOCHASTIC_COMPLETION_SOURCE_PRIVILEGED

Frozen NFC contains a qualifying principle satisfying P1–P8 that privileges genuine K2 over K1 at the relevant universal/history scope.

C__COMPLETION_CLASS_PARITY_PRESERVED_AT_FROZEN_SCOPE

No qualifying parity-breaking principle is source-forced, and a conservative deterministic/stochastic extension pair can be maintained consistently with frozen NFC at the audited scope.

D__ONLY_CONDITIONAL_OR_BRANCH_LOCAL_ASYMMETRY_FOUND

NFC contains deterministic- or stochastic-looking branch mechanisms, but their authority depends on branch-specific hypotheses, model data, or scoped bridges and does not lift to the universal actualization frontier.

Use D only if such asymmetry prevents a clean universal conservative-extension parity argument. Otherwise branch-local asymmetries are secondary observations under C.

E__UNDERDETERMINED_AT_CURRENT_SOURCE_SCOPE

The source contains unresolved ambiguity or incomplete authority structure that blocks a justified A–D classification.

F__FORMAL_REPAIR_REQUIRED

A contradiction, authority defect, or source inconsistency must be repaired before the parity question can be responsibly adjudicated.

## 10. Acceptance standard

Outcome A or B requires an explicit source-bound parity-breaking argument plus failure of the opposite-class conservative extension.

Mere prevalence of deterministic equations or stochastic notation is insufficient.

Outcome C requires more than failure to find a selector. It requires a constructive parity/independence argument, preferably by exhibiting two distinct completion extensions that leave frozen NFC theorem truth unchanged while differing in actualization class.
